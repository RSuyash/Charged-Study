# 4.4 Drug Delivery Systems

Drug delivery systems are technologies designed to transport therapeutic agents within the body to achieve a desired therapeutic effect while minimizing side effects. They aim to control the rate, time, and location of drug release, improving efficacy, safety, and patient compliance. Various systems exist, ranging from conventional dosage forms to advanced targeted and controlled-release technologies.
