# 4.6 Chemoinformatics

Chemoinformatics (Chemical Informatics) applies computational and informational techniques to solve problems in chemistry. It involves managing, analyzing, and visualizing chemical data (structures, properties, reactions) to accelerate research, particularly in drug discovery, materials science, and chemical synthesis.
