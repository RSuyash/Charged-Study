# 4.2 Drug Discovery and Development

Drug discovery and development is a long, complex, costly, and high-risk process aimed at identifying, developing, and bringing new therapeutic agents to market to treat diseases. It integrates knowledge from chemistry, biology, pharmacology, medicine, and regulatory science. This section covers the historical evolution, modern strategies like targeting and combinatorial approaches, and rational design principles.
