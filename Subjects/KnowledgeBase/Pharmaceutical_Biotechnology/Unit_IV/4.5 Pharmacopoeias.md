# 4.5 Pharmacopoeias

Pharmacopoeias are official compendia establishing standards for the quality, purity, strength, and identity of medicines. They are essential legal and scientific references for pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality control, and regulation, ensuring the safety and efficacy of drugs available to the public.
