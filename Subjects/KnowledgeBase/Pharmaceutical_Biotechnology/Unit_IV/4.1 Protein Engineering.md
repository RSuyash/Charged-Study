# 4.1 Protein Engineering

Protein engineering involves designing and constructing novel proteins or modifying existing ones to achieve altered or enhanced properties and functions. It leverages principles from biochemistry, molecular biology, genetics, and computational modeling to tailor proteins for specific applications, particularly in therapeutics and biotechnology.
