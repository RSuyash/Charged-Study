# 2.4 Microbial Resistance to Antibiotics and Antimicrobial Agents

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is the ability of microorganisms (like bacteria, fungi, viruses, parasites) to withstand the effects of antimicrobial drugs designed to kill them or inhibit their growth. AMR is a major global public health threat, complicating treatments and increasing morbidity, mortality, and healthcare costs. This section explores the different types of resistance and the underlying mechanisms bacteria employ to resist antibiotics.
