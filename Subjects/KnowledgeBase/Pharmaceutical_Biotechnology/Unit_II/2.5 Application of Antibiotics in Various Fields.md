# 2.5 Application of Antibiotics in Various Fields

Antibiotics, primarily known for treating bacterial infections in humans, have a surprisingly broad range of applications across diverse fields, leveraging their ability to selectively inhibit or kill microorganisms or, in some cases, their other biological activities.
