# 2.2 General Characteristics of an Antimicrobial Drug

Understanding the general characteristics of antimicrobial drugs is crucial for their effective and safe use in treating infections. Key characteristics include their spectrum of activity, pharmacokinetic properties (how the body handles the drug), and potential toxicity or side effects.
