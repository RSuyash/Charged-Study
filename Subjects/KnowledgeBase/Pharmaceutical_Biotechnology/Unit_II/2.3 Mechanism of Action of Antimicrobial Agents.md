# 2.3 Mechanism of Action of Antimicrobial Agents

Antimicrobial agents kill or inhibit microbial growth by targeting specific, essential cellular processes. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for effective use and development of new therapies. Key mechanisms include interference with cell wall synthesis, protein synthesis, nucleic acid synthesis, and membrane function.
