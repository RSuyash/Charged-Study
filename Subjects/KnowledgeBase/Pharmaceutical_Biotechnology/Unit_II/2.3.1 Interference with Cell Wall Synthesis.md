# 2.3.1 Interference with Cell Wall Synthesis

**Overview:**
The bacterial cell wall, primarily composed of peptidoglycan, is crucial for maintaining cell shape and integrity against osmotic stress. It's an excellent target for antibiotics as it's unique to bacteria and absent in eukaryotes. Inhibiting its synthesis leads to weakened walls and cell lysis, especially in growing bacteria.
