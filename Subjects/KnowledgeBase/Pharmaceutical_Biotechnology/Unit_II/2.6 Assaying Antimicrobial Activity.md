# 2.6 Assaying Antimicrobial Activity

Evaluating the effectiveness of antimicrobial agents against specific microorganisms is fundamental in microbiology, clinical diagnostics, and drug discovery. Various laboratory methods, known as antimicrobial susceptibility tests (AST) or microbial assays, are used to determine the potency and spectrum of activity of these agents.
