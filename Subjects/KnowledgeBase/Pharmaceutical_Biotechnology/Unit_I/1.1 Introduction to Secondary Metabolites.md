# 1.1 Introduction to Secondary Metabolites

This section introduces secondary metabolites (specialized metabolites), organic compounds synthesized by organisms like plants, fungi, and bacteria. Unlike primary metabolites essential for basic life functions, secondary metabolites mediate ecological interactions and enhance survival. This introduction covers their definition, characteristics, history, differences from primary metabolites, functions, and significance, particularly in the pharmaceutical industry.
