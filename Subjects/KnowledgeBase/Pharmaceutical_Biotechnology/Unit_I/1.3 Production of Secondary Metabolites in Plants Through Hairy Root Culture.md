# 1.3 Production of Secondary Metabolites in Plants Through Hairy Root Culture

Hairy root culture is a plant biotechnology technique using roots transformed by *Agrobacterium rhizogenes* for efficient secondary metabolite production. This section covers the principles, establishment, maintenance, advantages, and applications of this method.
