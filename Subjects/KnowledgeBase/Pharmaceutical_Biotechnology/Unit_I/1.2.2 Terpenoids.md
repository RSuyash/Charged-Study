# 1.2.2 Terpenoids

**Overview:**
Terpenoids (isoprenoids) are a large, diverse class of organic compounds derived from five-carbon **isoprene** units (IPP and DMAPP). They play critical roles in plant defense, pollinator attraction, and ecological interactions. Many have significant medicinal applications.

**Structure:**
Classified based on the number of C5 isoprene units:
-   **Monoterpenes (C10):** 2 units (e.g., limonene, menthol, geraniol). Often volatile components of essential oils.
-   **Sesquiterpenes (C15):** 3 units (e.g., farnesene, artemisinin). Also common in essential oils.
-   **Diterpenes (C20):** 4 units (e.g., taxol/paclitaxel, gibberellins).
-   **Triterpenes (C30):** 6 units (e.g., squalene - precursor to steroids, saponins).
-   **Tetraterpenes (C40):** 8 units (e.g., carotenoids like β-carotene, lycopene).
-   **Polyterpenes:** Many units (e.g., natural rubber).

*Isoprene Rule:* Terpenoids are often assembled via head-to-tail linkage of isoprene units.

**Sources:**
Widely distributed in plants (especially essential oils), also found in fungi, insects, marine organisms.
-   Peppermint (*Mentha piperita*): Menthol.
-   Lemon (*Citrus limon*): Limonene.
-   Sweet wormwood (*Artemisia annua*): Artemisinin.
-   Pacific yew (*Taxus brevifolia*): Paclitaxel (Taxol).

**Examples and Pharmacological Activities:**

1.  **Artemisinin:** Sesquiterpene lactone. Potent antimalarial (generates free radicals damaging parasite).
2.  **Paclitaxel (Taxol):** Complex diterpene. Anticancer drug (stabilizes microtubules, inhibits mitosis).
3.  **Limonene:** Cyclic monoterpene. Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, potential anticancer (induces apoptosis). Flavoring agent.
4.  **Squalene:** Linear triterpene. Emollient in cosmetics, cholesterol precursor, immunomodulatory effects.
5.  **Geraniol:** Acyclic monoterpene alcohol. Antimicrobial, fragrance/flavoring.
6.  **Menthol:** Cyclic monoterpene alcohol. Cooling sensation, topical analgesic, respiratory relief.

**Biosynthesis:**
Two main pathways produce the isoprene precursors (IPP and DMAPP):
1.  **Mevalonate Pathway (MVA):** Cytosolic/ER pathway. Starts from acetyl-CoA. Produces precursors for sesquiterpenes, triterpenes (sterols), polyterpenes.
2.  **Methylerythritol Phosphate Pathway (MEP) / Non-mevalonate Pathway:** Plastidial pathway. Starts from pyruvate and glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate. Produces precursors for monoterpenes, diterpenes, tetraterpenes (carotenoids).

**Medicinal Applications:**
-   **Anticancer Agents:** Paclitaxel.
-   **Antimalarials:** Artemisinin and derivatives.
-   **Antimicrobial Properties:** Many essential oil components (e.g., geraniol, thymol).
-   **Anti-inflammatory Effects:** Limonene, α-bisabolol.
-   **Flavoring Agents & Aromatherapy:** Menthol, limonene, linalool.

**Future Directions:**
-   Exploring novel terpenoids for drug discovery (e.g., against resistant pathogens).
-   Synthetic biology/metabolic engineering to enhance production in microbes or plants.
-   Investigating synergistic effects of terpenoid combinations.
