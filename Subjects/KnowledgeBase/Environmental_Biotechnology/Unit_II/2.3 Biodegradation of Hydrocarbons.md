# 2.3 Biodegradation of Hydrocarbons

Hydrocarbons (compounds of C and H only) are major components of petroleum products and significant environmental pollutants. [[2.1 Biodegradation Definition and Concept|Biodegradation]] by [[Microorganisms]] is a crucial natural process for their removal.

## [[2.3.1 Mechanisms and Pathways (Hydrocarbons)]]

Mechanisms vary depending on hydrocarbon type (alkanes, alkenes, aromatics, PAHs) and conditions (aerobic/anaerobic).

-   **Aerobic:** Generally faster/more complete. Uses O₂ as electron acceptor and often for initial activation (oxygenases).
    -   *Alkanes:* Terminal oxidation (monooxygenase → alcohol → aldehyde → fatty acid → β-oxidation).
    -   *Alkenes:* Epoxidation (mono-/di-oxygenase) → Epoxide hydrolysis → Further metabolism.
    -   *Aromatics (Benzene, Toluene):* Dioxygenation → *cis*-Dihydrodiols → Dehydrogenation → Catechols → [[2.2.1.2 Ring Cleavage Mechanisms (Aerobic)|Ring Cleavage]] (*ortho* or *meta*).
    -   *PAHs:* Similar initial steps, sequential ring cleavage. More complex/recalcitrant with increasing size/angularity.
-   **Anaerobic:** Slower, uses alternative electron acceptors (NO₃⁻, SO₄²⁻, Fe³⁺, CO₂).
    -   *Activation:* Often involves addition to fumarate (glycyl radical enzymes).
    -   *Oxidation Coupled to Electron Acceptor Reduction:* e.g., Nitrate reduction (denitrification), Sulfate reduction, Iron(III) reduction.
    -   *[[Methanogenesis]]:* Terminal process for smaller intermediates (acetate, H₂/CO₂).
    -   *Aromatic Anaerobic Degradation:* Complex, involves activation (fumarate addition), ring reduction, reductive cleavage.

## [[2.3.2 Microorganisms Involved (Hydrocarbons)]]

Diverse bacteria, fungi, and archaea participate.

-   **Aerobic:**
    -   *Bacteria:* *Pseudomonas*, *Alcanivorax* (marine alkanes), *Rhodococcus*, *Sphingomonas* (PAHs), *Mycobacterium*, *Nocardia*, *Burkholderia*, *Bacillus*.
    -   *Fungi:* Yeasts (*Candida*, *Yarrowia*), Filamentous Fungi (*Aspergillus*, *Penicillium*, White-rot fungi for PAHs).
-   **Anaerobic:**
    -   *Bacteria:* Sulfate-reducers (SRB - *Desulfosarcina*), Denitrifiers (*Pseudomonas*), Iron-reducers (IRB - *Geobacter*).
    -   *[[Methanogenic Archaea]]:* Crucial terminal role (consume acetate, H₂/CO₂).

## [[2.3.3 Applications and Case Studies (Hydrocarbons)]]

Applied in [[Bioremediation]] and oil spill cleanup.

-   **Bioremediation:**
    -   *Ex-situ:* Treatment in [[Bioreactors]], [[Landfarming]], [[Biopiles]].
    -   *In-situ:* [[Biostimulation]] (add nutrients/O₂), [[Bioaugmentation]] (add specific microbes).
-   **Oil Spill Cleanup:** Natural attenuation, biostimulation (nutrients), bioaugmentation. Dispersants used sometimes (impact debated).
-   **Case Studies:** Exxon Valdez, Deepwater Horizon, industrial site cleanups.

Hydrocarbon biodegradation is a key environmental biotechnology tool.
