# 2.3.2 Microorganisms Involved (Hydrocarbons)

A diverse array of [[Microorganisms]] (bacteria, fungi, archaea) participate in the [[2.3 Biodegradation of Hydrocarbons|biodegradation of hydrocarbons]], utilizing different [[2.3.1 Mechanisms and Pathways (Hydrocarbons)|pathways]] under aerobic or anaerobic conditions.

## 1. Aerobic Hydrocarbon-Degrading Microorganisms

-   **[[Bacteria (Aerobic Wastewater Treatment)|Bacteria]] (Dominant):**
    -   ***Pseudomonas*:** Highly versatile; degrade alkanes, aromatics, PAHs.
    -   ***Alcanivorax*:** Marine specialists for alkane degradation (oil spills).
    -   ***Rhodococcus*:** Broad range degradation (alkanes, aromatics, PAHs).
    *   ***Sphingomonas*:** Degrade PAHs and other aromatics.
    *   ***Mycobacterium*:** Degrade alkanes, aromatics.
    *   ***Nocardia*:** Degrade alkanes, aromatics.
    *   ***Burkholderia*:** Degrade aromatics, other pollutants.
    *   ***Bacillus*:** Degrade alkanes, other hydrocarbons.
-   **[[Fungi (Aerobic Wastewater Treatment)|Fungi]]:**
    -   *Yeasts:* *Candida*, *Yarrowia* (degrade alkanes, etc.).
    *   *Filamentous Fungi:* *Aspergillus*, *Penicillium*, *Pleurotus*. White-rot fungi are notable PAH degraders via ligninolytic enzymes.

## 2. Anaerobic Hydrocarbon-Degrading Microorganisms

-   **[[Bacteria (Anaerobic Wastewater Treatment)|Bacteria]] (Dominant):**
    -   *Sulfate-Reducing Bacteria (SRB):* Use SO₄²⁻ as electron acceptor. Degrade alkanes/aromatics. Examples: *Desulfosarcina*, *Desulfobacter*.
    *   *Denitrifying Bacteria:* Use NO₃⁻ as electron acceptor. Degrade alkanes/aromatics. Examples: *Pseudomonas*, *Acinetobacter*.
    *   *Iron-Reducing Bacteria (IRB):* Use Fe³⁺ as electron acceptor. Degrade alkanes/aromatics. Examples: *Geobacter*, *Shewanella*.
-   **[[Methanogenic Archaea]]:**
    -   *Role:* Crucial terminal role. Consume acetate, H₂, CO₂ produced by bacteria, facilitating overall anaerobic degradation via [[Methanogenesis]]. Do not directly degrade complex hydrocarbons.
-   **[[Fungi (Anaerobic Wastewater Treatment)|Anaerobic Fungi]]:**
    -   *Role:* Primarily degrade plant biomass (lignocellulose). Limited role in direct hydrocarbon degradation.

The specific microbial community present and active at a contaminated site depends heavily on the type of hydrocarbon, environmental conditions (O₂, electron acceptors, nutrients, pH, temp), and history of contamination.
