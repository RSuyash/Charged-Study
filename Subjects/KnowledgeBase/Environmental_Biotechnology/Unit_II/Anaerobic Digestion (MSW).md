# Anaerobic Digestion (MSW)

[[Anaerobic Digestion]] (AD) is an [[1.1.2.2 Anaerobic Biological Processes|anaerobic biological process]] used as one of the [[2.4.3 Biological Treatment Methods (MSW)|biological treatment methods]] for the organic fraction of [[2.4 Municipal Solid Waste Management|MSW]]. [[1.2.2 Anaerobic Microorganisms|Microorganisms]] break down organic waste in the absence of oxygen, producing [[Biogas]] (methane + CO₂) and **digestate** (stabilized sludge). AD is particularly effective for wet organic wastes like food waste.

## Process Design

1.  **Feedstock Preparation:** Organic fraction from [[2.4.2 Waste Segregation and Recycling|source segregation]] may need size reduction, sorting, removal of contaminants. Slurry preparation often needed.
2.  **Anaerobic Digesters:** Enclosed reactors maintaining anaerobic conditions and optimal temperature.
    *   *Types:*
        *   *Mesophilic:* 30-40°C (more stable, common for MSW).
        *   *Thermophilic:* 50-60°C (faster, better pathogen kill, less stable, higher energy input).
        *   *Single-Stage:* All AD stages in one reactor.
        *   *Two-Stage:* Separates hydrolysis/acidogenesis from acetogenesis/methanogenesis for potentially better control/efficiency.
3.  **Biogas Collection/Utilization:** Biogas collected, cleaned (e.g., H₂S removal), used for heat/power generation (renewable energy).
4.  **Digestate Management:** Stabilized solid/liquid residue (digestate) requires dewatering. Can be used as soil amendment/fertilizer after processing/quality checks.

## Stages of Anaerobic Digestion

Follows the standard four stages:
1.  [[Hydrolysis]]
2.  [[Acidogenesis]] / [[Fermentative Pathways]]
3.  [[Acetogenesis]] (often involving [[Syntrophic Bacteria]])
4.  [[Methanogenesis]] (by [[Methanogenic Archaea]])

## Operational Parameters and Control

-   **Temperature:** Mesophilic or Thermophilic range maintained.
-   **pH:** Optimal near neutral (6.5-7.5) for methanogens. Control crucial to prevent acidification.
-   **Organic Loading Rate (OLR):** Controlled to avoid overloading.
-   **Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT):** Typically 15-30 days (mesophilic).
-   **Mixing:** Ensure uniform conditions, prevent stratification.
-   **Nutrient Balance:** Ensure sufficient trace nutrients.
-   **Inhibitor Monitoring:** Check for ammonia, heavy metals, etc.
