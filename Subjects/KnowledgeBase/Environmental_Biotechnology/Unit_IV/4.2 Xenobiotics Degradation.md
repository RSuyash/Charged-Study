# 4.2 Xenobiotics Degradation

This section focuses on the specific mechanisms and pathways involved in the [[Biodegradation]] of key classes of [[Xenobiotics]], particularly [[4.2.1 Pesticide Degradation|pesticides]] and [[4.2.2 Herbicide Degradation|herbicides]].

Understanding these pathways is crucial for developing effective [[Bioremediation]] strategies.
