# 4.3 Metabolism of Xenobiotics

This section focuses on the enzymatic processes by which organisms, particularly mammals, metabolize [[Xenobiotics]]. These processes are crucial for detoxification and elimination, but can also sometimes lead to bioactivation (formation of toxic metabolites).

Key aspects:

-   [[4.3.1 Cytochrome P450 System]]: A major enzyme system involved in Phase I metabolism.
-   [[4.3.2 Phase I Reactions of Xenobiotic Metabolism]]: Initial reactions (oxidation, hydrolysis, reduction) that modify xenobiotics.
-   [[4.3.3 Phase II Reactions of Xenobiotic Metabolism]]: Conjugation reactions that further modify and facilitate excretion.

Understanding these metabolic pathways is essential for predicting drug interactions, assessing toxicity, and designing safer chemicals.
