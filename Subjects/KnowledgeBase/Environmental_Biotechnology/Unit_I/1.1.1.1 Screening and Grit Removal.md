# 1.1.1.1 Screening and Grit Removal

Screening and grit removal are the initial physical processes in [[1.1.1 Primary Treatment]] of wastewater, designed to remove large solids and heavy inorganic materials, respectively.

![Fig. Primary treatment process (a) screening process (b) grit chamber and (c) wastewater skimming tank.](<../../../V2/Environmental Biotechnology/EB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 2.png>)
*Fig. Primary treatment: (a) screening, (b) grit chamber, (c) skimming tank.*

## Screening

-   **Principle:** First unit operation. Removes large floating/suspended objects (rags, sticks, paper, plastics) by passing wastewater through screens. Prevents clogging/damage to downstream equipment.
-   **Process Design:** Screens made of parallel bars or mesh.
    -   *Coarse Screens:* Larger openings (> 6 mm), remove large debris. Manually or mechanically cleaned.
    -   *Fine Screens:* Smaller openings (1.5 - 6 mm), remove smaller solids. Usually mechanically cleaned.
-   **Operation/Control:**
    -   *Screen Size:* Selected based on wastewater characteristics and downstream needs.
    -   *Cleaning Frequency:* Regular cleaning (manual or automated via timers/headloss sensors) prevents clogging.
    -   *Disposal:* Screenings typically landfilled or incinerated after dewatering.

## Grit Removal

-   **Principle:** Separates heavier inorganic solids (sand, gravel, eggshells - "grit") from wastewater via gravity. Grit causes abrasion/wear on equipment and accumulates in tanks.
-   **Process Design:** Systems designed to reduce flow velocity, allowing grit to settle while keeping lighter organic solids suspended.
    -   *Grit Channels:* Long, narrow channels (historical, less common now due to space/odor). Velocity ~0.3 m/s.
    -   *Aerated Grit Chambers:* More compact. Air creates spiral flow, promoting grit settling while keeping organics suspended.
    *   *Vortex Grit Separators:* Use centrifugal force in a cylindrical chamber. Grit forced outwards and settles.
-   **Operation/Control:**
    -   *Flow Velocity/Aeration Rate:* Controlled to optimize grit settling without removing excessive organics.
    -   *Grit Washing/Disposal:* Removed grit often washed to remove organics before landfill disposal.

These initial steps are crucial for protecting downstream processes and improving the overall efficiency of [[1.1 Domestic (Municipal) and Industrial Waste Water Treatments|wastewater treatment]].
