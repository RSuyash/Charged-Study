# Bacteria (Anaerobic Wastewater Treatment)

[[1.2.2 Anaerobic Microorganisms|Anaerobic bacteria]] form a complex consortium essential for the stepwise breakdown of organic matter in [[1.1.2.2 Anaerobic Biological Processes|anaerobic wastewater treatment]] and [[Anaerobic Digestion|digestion]]. They perform the initial and intermediate stages leading up to [[Methanogenesis]].

## Key Functional Groups

1.  **Hydrolytic Bacteria:**
    *   **Role:** Perform the first step, **hydrolysis**. Secrete extracellular enzymes (cellulases, proteases, lipases) to break down complex polymers (carbohydrates, proteins, lipids) into soluble monomers (sugars, amino acids, fatty acids).
    *   **Examples:** *Clostridium*, *Bacteroides*, *Bifidobacterium*, *Streptococcus*.

2.  **Acidogenic Bacteria (Fermenters):**
    *   **Role:** Perform **acidogenesis** (fermentation). Convert monomers into a mixture of volatile fatty acids (VFAs - acetate, propionate, butyrate), alcohols, H₂, and CO₂.
    *   **Examples:** *Escherichia*, *Enterobacter*, *Lactobacillus*, *Clostridium* (some species are both hydrolytic and acidogenic).

3.  **Acetogenic Bacteria:**
    *   **Role:** Perform **acetogenesis**. Convert VFAs (except acetate) and alcohols produced by acidogens into acetate, H₂, and CO₂. This step is crucial for providing substrates for methanogens.
    *   **Syntrophy:** Acetogenesis of propionate and butyrate is often thermodynamically unfavorable unless H₂ levels are kept very low. Therefore, acetogens typically live in close **syntrophic** association with hydrogen-consuming [[Methanogenic Archaea]].
    *   **Examples:** [[Syntrophic Bacteria]] like *Syntrophobacter* (propionate oxidizer), *Syntrophomonas* (butyrate oxidizer).

4.  **Syntrophic Acetate Oxidation Bacteria (SAOB):**
    *   **Role:** Under certain conditions (e.g., high temperature, high ammonia, low acetate), some bacteria can oxidize acetate to H₂ and CO₂. This process is also syntrophic, requiring coupling with hydrogenotrophic methanogens.
    *   **Significance:** Can be important in thermophilic digestion or when acetoclastic methanogenesis is inhibited.

These bacterial groups work sequentially and often interdependently (especially via syntrophy) to convert complex organic matter into simple substrates usable by methanogenic archaea.
