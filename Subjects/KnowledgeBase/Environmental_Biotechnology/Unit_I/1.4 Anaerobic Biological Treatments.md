# 1.4 Anaerobic Biological Treatments

[[1.1.2.2 Anaerobic Biological Processes|Anaerobic biological treatments]] are used for [[1.1 Domestic (Municipal) and Industrial Waste Water Treatments|wastewater]] with high organic loads or for sludge stabilization. They operate in the absence of oxygen, utilizing [[1.2.2 Anaerobic Microorganisms]] to break down organic matter, producing [[Biogas]] (methane + CO₂) and reducing sludge volume.

![Fig. Anaerobic Biological Treatment](<../../../V2/Environmental Biotechnology/EB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 8.png>)
*Fig. Anaerobic Biological Treatment*

    -   [[1.4.1.1 Principles and Process Design (AnMBRs)]]

    -   [[1.4.2.1 Principles and Process Design (PBRs)]]
    -   [[1.4.2.2 Operational Parameters and Control (PBRs)]]
-   [[Anaerobic Digestion|Anaerobic Digesters]] (Covered conceptually under 1.1.2.2)
-   [[UASB Reactors]] (Upflow Anaerobic Sludge Blanket) (Covered conceptually under 1.1.2.2)

These processes offer benefits like energy recovery but require careful control of parameters like temperature, pH, and loading rates.
