# 1.2.3 Principles of Growth and Plasmid-Borne Metabolic Activities

Understanding microbial growth principles and the role of plasmids is fundamental to managing [[1.2 Important Microorganisms in Waste Water Treatment|microorganisms in wastewater treatment]].

## Principles of Microbial Growth

-   **Growth Definition:** Increase in the *number* of microbial cells in a population.
-   **[[Growth Curve|Growth Curve (Batch Culture)]]:** Describes population growth over time.
    1.  *Lag Phase:* Adaptation period, little/no growth.
    2.  *Exponential (Log) Phase:* Rapid, constant growth rate (maximum). Cells actively dividing. [[3.3.2.2 Measuring Parameters of Growth|Doubling Time (Td)]] is constant.
    3.  *Stationary Phase:* Growth rate = 0 (division rate = death rate). Nutrient limitation, waste accumulation. Max cell density.
    4.  *Death Phase:* Viable cell count declines.
-   **Factors Affecting Growth:**
    *   *Nutrients:* C, N, P, S, minerals, growth factors.
    *   *Temperature:* Optimal, minimum, maximum ranges (Mesophilic: 20-40°C, Thermophilic: 50-60°C common in treatment).
    *   *pH:* Optimal range (often near neutral 6.5-7.5).
    *   *Oxygen:* Required by [[1.2.1 Aerobic Microorganisms|aerobes]], absent for [[1.2.2 Anaerobic Microorganisms|anaerobes]].
    *   *Water Availability (Water Activity).*
    *   *Osmotic Pressure/Salinity.*
    *   *Toxic Substances:* Heavy metals, high pollutant concentrations.

## Plasmid-Borne Metabolic Activities

-   **[[Plasmids]]:** Extrachromosomal, self-replicating DNA molecules in bacteria, archaea, some fungi. Not essential for basic survival but carry beneficial genes.
-   **Plasmid-Borne Genes in Wastewater Treatment:**
    *   **Antibiotic Resistance:** Common on plasmids, spread via HGT is a public health concern in wastewater systems.
    *   **Metal Resistance:** Genes enabling tolerance/detoxification of heavy metals (important for industrial wastewater).
    *   **Degradative Genes:** Encode enzymes for degrading specific organic pollutants (pesticides, hydrocarbons, industrial chemicals). Found in genera like *Pseudomonas*. Enhance metabolic versatility.
    *   **Virulence Factors:** (In pathogens) Can be plasmid-borne.
-   **Horizontal Gene Transfer (HGT):** Plasmids spread between microbes via:
    *   *Conjugation:* Direct cell-to-cell transfer via pili.
    *   *Transformation:* Uptake of free DNA from environment.
    *   *Transduction:* Transfer via bacteriophages (viruses).
-   **Significance:** HGT of plasmids rapidly disseminates traits like resistance and degradation capabilities within microbial communities in treatment plants, influencing process efficiency and effluent quality (both positively and negatively).

Understanding growth kinetics helps optimize reactor design (HRT, SRT) and operating conditions. Understanding plasmid roles helps explain microbial adaptation to pollutants and the spread of resistance.
