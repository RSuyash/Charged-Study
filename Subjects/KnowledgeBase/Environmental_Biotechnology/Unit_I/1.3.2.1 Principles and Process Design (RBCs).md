# 1.3.2.1 Principles and Process Design (RBCs)

This note details the core principles and design components of [[Rotating Biological Contactors (RBCs)]].

## Principles of RBCs

-   **Biofilm-Based Process:** RBCs are [[1.3 Aerobic Biological Treatments|aerobic treatment]] systems where [[1.2.1 Aerobic Microorganisms|microorganisms]] grow as a biofilm on rotating disc surfaces.
-   **Attached Growth System:** Microbes are immobilized on the disc media, contrasting with the suspended growth in [[Activated Sludge Process (ASP)|ASP]].
-   **Alternating Exposure:** As discs rotate (typically 1-2 rpm), the biofilm is alternately submerged in wastewater (for substrate uptake) and exposed to air (for oxygen uptake).
-   **Oxygen Transfer:** Occurs primarily during atmospheric exposure.
-   **Biomass Sloughing:** Excess biofilm naturally detaches (sloughs off) due to shear forces and gravity. Sloughed biomass is removed in a downstream secondary clarifier.

## Process Design Components

1.  **RBC Unit:**
    *   *Discs:* Series of closely spaced circular discs (plastic, e.g., HDPE, polystyrene) mounted on a horizontal shaft.
    *   *Shaft & Drive:* Horizontal shaft rotated by a motor and gear reducer system.
    *   *Tank:* Contains the wastewater through which the discs rotate.
    *   *Stages:* Often designed in multiple stages (3-4) in series for progressive [[BOD]] removal.
    *   *Submergence:* Typically ~40% of disc area submerged.
    *   *Media Surface Area:* Key design parameter; higher area = more biofilm = higher capacity.
    *   *Cover (Optional):* Reduces odors, controls algae, protects from weather.

2.  **Primary Clarification (Pre-treatment):** Essential upstream [[1.1.1 Primary Treatment]] (screening, sedimentation) to remove large solids that could damage discs or reduce efficiency.

3.  **Secondary Clarification (Post-treatment):** Required downstream to settle and remove sloughed biomass from the treated effluent.

## Key Design Parameters

-   **Hydraulic Loading Rate (HLR):** Wastewater flow rate per unit media surface area (e.g., 0.02-0.08 m³/m²·d). Lower HLR = better treatment (larger unit).
-   **Organic Loading Rate (OLR):** Mass of [[BOD]] applied per unit media surface area per time (e.g., 5-20 g BOD/m²·d). Influences efficiency.
-   **Number of Stages:** Typically 3-4 for desired BOD removal.
-   **Disc Diameter/Spacing:** Diameter typically 2-4 m. Spacing allows biofilm growth without clogging.
-   **Rotation Speed:** Typically 1-2 rpm.
-   **Media Type/Surface Area:** High surface area plastic media common.

RBCs provide a relatively simple, stable attached growth process for secondary wastewater treatment.
