# Bacteria (Aerobic Wastewater Treatment)

Bacteria are the primary and most important group of [[1.2.1 Aerobic Microorganisms]] responsible for the breakdown of organic matter and nutrient transformations in [[1.1.2.1 Aerobic Biological Processes|aerobic wastewater treatment]].

## Key Roles and Examples

-   **Heterotrophic Bacteria:**
    -   **Role:** Degrade a wide range of organic compounds (carbonaceous [[BOD]] removal) using organic carbon as both energy and carbon source. They form the bulk of the biomass in processes like the [[Activated Sludge Process (ASP)]].
    -   **Examples:**
        -   ***Pseudomonas*:** Versatile genus, degrades diverse organics (hydrocarbons, pesticides, industrial pollutants). Ubiquitous in soil/water.
        -   ***Zoogloea*:** Important floc-forming bacteria in ASP, contributing to biomass settling. Produces extracellular polymeric substances (EPS).
        -   ***Bacillus*:** Gram-positive, spore-forming. Some species degrade complex organics.
        -   ***Acinetobacter*:** Known for phosphorus accumulation, important in Enhanced Biological Phosphorus Removal (EBPR) processes.
        -   *Flavobacterium*, *Achromobacter*, *Alcaligenes*: Other common heterotrophs found in activated sludge.

-   **Nitrifying Bacteria (Chemolithoautotrophs):**
    -   **Role:** Carry out nitrification, the two-step aerobic oxidation of ammonia (NH₃) to nitrate (NO₃⁻). Crucial for nitrogen removal.
    -   **Steps & Examples:**
        1.  *Ammonia Oxidation:* Ammonia-Oxidizing Bacteria (AOB) oxidize ammonia to nitrite (NO₂⁻). Example: ***Nitrosomonas***.
            `NH₃ + O₂ → NO₂⁻ + H⁺ + H₂O` (Simplified)
        2.  *Nitrite Oxidation:* Nitrite-Oxidizing Bacteria (NOB) oxidize nitrite to nitrate (NO₃⁻). Example: ***Nitrobacter***.
            `NO₂⁻ + O₂ → NO₃⁻` (Simplified)
    -   **Conditions:** Nitrifiers are slow-growing and sensitive to environmental conditions (DO, pH, temperature, toxic substances). Require longer SRTs compared to heterotrophs.

The specific bacterial community composition in an aerobic treatment system is dynamic and influenced by influent wastewater characteristics and operational parameters.
