# 1.1 Domestic (Municipal) and Industrial Waste Water Treatments

**Wastewater treatment** is crucial for environmental protection and public health, removing contaminants before discharge or reuse. Wastewater sources are broadly categorized as domestic (municipal) and industrial.

-   **Domestic (Municipal) Wastewater:** Generated from residential, commercial, institutional sources. Primarily human waste, cleaning products, food scraps. Composition relatively consistent.
-   **Industrial Wastewater:** From manufacturing/industrial processes. Highly variable composition depending on industry (heavy metals, organic chemicals, oils, etc.). Often requires pre-treatment before municipal discharge.
