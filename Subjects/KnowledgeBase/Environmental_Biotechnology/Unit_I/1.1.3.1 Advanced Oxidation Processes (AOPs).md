# 1.1.3.1 Advanced Oxidation Processes (AOPs)

Advanced Oxidation Processes (AOPs) are a set of chemical [[1.1.3 Tertiary Treatment (Advanced Treatment)|tertiary treatment]] procedures used in [[1.1 Domestic (Municipal) and Industrial Waste Water Treatments|wastewater treatment]] to remove organic pollutants, especially recalcitrant and toxic compounds resistant to conventional biological treatment.

## Principle

AOPs generate highly reactive and non-selective **hydroxyl radicals (•OH)**. These radicals rapidly oxidize a wide range of organic contaminants, breaking them down into smaller, less harmful molecules, potentially leading to complete mineralization (CO₂ and H₂O).

## Common AOPs

-   **Ozonation (O₃):** Ozone acts as a strong oxidant directly or decomposes to form •OH radicals. Effective for disinfection, color removal, degradation of some organics.
-   **UV/Hydrogen Peroxide (UV/H₂O₂):** UV radiation activates H₂O₂ to generate •OH radicals. Effective for removing pharmaceuticals, endocrine disruptors, etc.
-   **Fenton Process (Fe²⁺/H₂O₂):** Ferrous ions (Fe²⁺) catalyze H₂O₂ decomposition to produce •OH radicals. Effective for recalcitrant industrial wastewater, operates best at acidic pH.
-   **Photo-Fenton (UV/Fe²⁺/H₂O₂):** Combines Fenton process with UV light to enhance •OH radical generation.
-   **Titanium Dioxide Photocatalysis (TiO₂/UV):** UV light activates TiO₂ (semiconductor photocatalyst) to generate electron-hole pairs, which react with water/oxygen to produce •OH radicals and other reactive oxygen species.

## Operational Parameters and Control

-   **Oxidant Dosage (O₃, H₂O₂):** Optimize for effective removal without excess consumption/byproducts.
-   **UV Intensity:** Sufficient intensity needed for UV-based AOPs.
-   **pH:** Can significantly affect efficiency (e.g., Fenton needs acidic pH).
-   **Reaction Time:** Sufficient time required for oxidation.
-   **Catalyst Concentration (Fenton, Photocatalysis):** Optimize catalyst level.
-   **Turbidity/Suspended Solids:** Can interfere with UV penetration; pre-treatment may be needed.

AOPs are powerful tools for treating challenging wastewater streams containing persistent organic pollutants.
