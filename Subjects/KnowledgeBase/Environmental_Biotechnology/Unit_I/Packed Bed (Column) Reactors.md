# Packed Bed (Column) Reactors (Anaerobic)

Packed Bed Reactors (PBRs), specifically anaerobic PBRs, are a type of [[1.4 Anaerobic Biological Treatments|anaerobic biological treatment]] system used for wastewater treatment. They are biofilm-based, attached growth systems.

![Fig. Schematic (A) and picture of the packed bed column (B)...](<../../../V2/Environmental Biotechnology/EB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 10.png>)
*Fig. Schematic (A) and picture (B) of a packed bed column.*

## [[1.4.2.1 Principles and Process Design (PBRs)]]

-   **Principles:** [[1.2.2 Anaerobic Microorganisms|Anaerobic microorganisms]] grow as a biofilm on packing media within a column reactor. Wastewater flows through the packed bed (upflow or downflow, upflow common for anaerobic), contacting the biofilm where organic matter is degraded anaerobically, producing [[Biogas]]. The fixed biofilm allows for high biomass concentrations and long SRTs.
-   **Process Design:** Vertical column packed with inert media (random or structured). Includes wastewater distribution, effluent collection, and biogas collection systems. May require pre-treatment and optional effluent recirculation.

## [[1.4.2.2 Operational Parameters and Control (PBRs)]]

-   **Key Parameters:** Temperature (Mesophilic/Thermophilic), pH (neutral), VFAs, Alkalinity, Hydraulic Loading Rate (HLR), Organic Loading Rate (OLR), Recirculation Ratio (if used), Biogas production, Pressure Drop (indicates clogging).
-   **Control:** Temperature/pH control, flow control (HLR/OLR), recirculation control, biogas monitoring. Clogging is a major operational challenge, potentially requiring backwashing or media cleaning.

Anaerobic PBRs are effective for treating certain types of wastewater, particularly those with moderate organic loads, offering advantages of attached growth systems like biomass retention and stability.
