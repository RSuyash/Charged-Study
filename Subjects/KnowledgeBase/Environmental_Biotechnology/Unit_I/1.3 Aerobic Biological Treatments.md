# 1.3 Aerobic Biological Treatments

[[1.1.2.1 Aerobic Biological Processes|Aerobic biological treatments]] are central to [[1.1.2 Secondary Treatment|secondary wastewater treatment]], effectively removing biodegradable organic matter using [[1.2.1 Aerobic Microorganisms|microorganisms]] in oxygen-rich environments.

Key aerobic treatment methods include:

-   [[Activated Sludge Process (ASP)]]
    -   [[1.3.1.1 Principles and Process Design (ASP)]]
    -   [[1.3.1.2 Operational Parameters and Control (ASP)]]
-   [[Rotating Biological Contactors (RBCs)]]
    -   [[1.3.2.1 Principles and Process Design (RBCs)]]
    -   [[1.3.2.2 Operational Parameters and Control (RBCs)]]
-   [[Trickling Filters (TFs)]] *(Note: Detailed in source but not explicitly numbered under 1.3 in the provided text structure)*
-   [[Aerated Lagoons]] *(Note: Mentioned under 1.1.2.1 but not detailed under 1.3)*

These processes rely on supplying sufficient oxygen to allow microorganisms to oxidize organic pollutants efficiently.
