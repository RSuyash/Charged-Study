# 1.4.1.1 Principles and Process Design (AnMBRs)

This note details the core principles and design components of [[Anaerobic Membrane Bioreactors (AnMBRs)]].

## Principles of AnMBRs

-   **Combined Process:** Integrates [[Anaerobic Digestion]] (organic breakdown, [[Biogas]] production) with [[1.1.3.2 Membrane Filtration|membrane filtration]] (UF/MF) for solid-liquid separation.
-   **Complete Biomass Retention:** Membranes provide absolute barrier, retaining all [[1.2.2 Anaerobic Microorganisms|anaerobic biomass]] (bacteria, archaea) within the reactor.
    -   Allows high biomass concentrations (high MLSS).
    -   Decouples Solids Retention Time (SRT) from Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT), enabling long SRTs (days/weeks/months) even with shorter HRTs (hours/days). Long SRT enhances digestion of complex organics and reduces sludge production.
-   **High Effluent Quality:** Membrane filtration produces permeate (effluent) free of suspended solids, bacteria, and often viruses (depending on pore size), suitable for [[Water Reuse|reuse]] or discharge.
-   **Air Lift Circulation (Specific Type):** In Air Lift AnMBRs, injected air in a riser section creates buoyancy-driven circulation (upflow in riser, downflow in downcomer). Provides gentle mixing and membrane scouring to reduce fouling, potentially lowering energy use compared to mechanical mixing.

## Process Design Components

1.  **Anaerobic Reactor with Membrane Module:**
    *   *Reactor Tank:* Vessel designed for anaerobic conditions.
    *   *Membrane Module(s):* Submerged directly in the reactor liquor. Common configurations:
        *   Hollow Fiber Membranes
        *   Flat Sheet Membranes
    *   *Permeate Collection System:* Piping and pumps to draw permeate through the membranes under suction.
    *   *Air Lift System (for Air Lift type):* Air diffusers at the bottom of a riser section.
    *   *Biogas Collection System:* Headspace collection and removal system.
    *   *Mixing System (if not Air Lift):* Mechanical mixers may be used in some AnMBR designs.

2.  **Pre-treatment (Optional):** Screening, grit removal may be needed for influent wastewater.

3.  **Permeate Pumping System:** Pumps for permeate extraction.

4.  **Biogas Handling System:** Collection, treatment (e.g., H₂S removal), utilization (energy generation) or flaring.

5.  **Chemical Dosing System (Optional):** For pH control (alkali addition), nutrient supplementation, or membrane chemical cleaning.

6.  **Sludge Wasting System (WAS):** To control SRT, although WAS rates are typically very low due to long SRT operation.

## Key Design Parameters

-   **Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT):** Time wastewater spends in reactor (e.g., 12-48 hours).
-   **Solids Retention Time (SRT):** Average time biomass stays in reactor (e.g., 20-50+ days). Controlled by WAS rate.
-   **Organic Loading Rate (OLR):** Mass COD/BOD per reactor volume per time. Key parameter for anaerobic digestion capacity.
-   **Membrane Flux:** Permeate flow rate per membrane area (e.g., 5-20 L/m²·h). Lower than aerobic MBRs due to higher solids/fouling potential.
-   **Transmembrane Pressure (TMP):** Pressure difference across membrane; monitored to indicate fouling.
-   **Air Flow Rate (Air Lift):** Optimized for mixing/scouring vs. energy.
-   **Membrane Pore Size:** UF (0.01-0.1 µm) or MF (0.1-0.2 µm).
-   **Temperature:** Mesophilic (30-40°C) or Thermophilic (50-60°C).

AnMBR design aims to integrate efficient anaerobic digestion with reliable membrane separation for high performance and effluent quality.
