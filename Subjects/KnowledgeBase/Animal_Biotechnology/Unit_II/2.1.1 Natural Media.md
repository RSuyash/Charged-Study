# 2.1.1 Natural Media

Natural media represent the earliest forms of [[Animal Cell Culture]] media. They are derived from biological sources and provide a complex, undefined mixture of nutrients and factors essential for cell growth. While less common today due to batch-to-batch variability and ethical concerns, they are historically significant.

This category includes:
-   [[Basal Salt Solutions (BSS)]]
-   [[Minimum Essential Medium (MEM)]] (Note: MEM is chemically defined but often used as a base for natural/serum-supplemented media)

Natural media often require supplementation with biological fluids like serum, lymph, or tissue extracts to support robust cell growth.
