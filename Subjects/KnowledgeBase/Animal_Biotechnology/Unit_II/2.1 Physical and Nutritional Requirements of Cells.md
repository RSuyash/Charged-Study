# 2.1 Physical and Nutritional Requirements of Cells

[[Animal Cell Culture]], the *in vitro* growth of animal cells, necessitates a carefully controlled environment mimicking *in vivo* physiological conditions. Meeting both physical and nutritional needs is crucial for cell survival, growth, proliferation, and function.
