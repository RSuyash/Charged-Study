# 2.2.2 Types of Cell Cultures

[[Animal Cell Culture|Animal cell cultures]] can be broadly classified based on their growth characteristics, origin, lifespan, and other features.

The main types include:

-   [[Monolayer Cultures]]: Cells grow attached to a surface in a single layer.
-   [[Suspension Cultures]]: Cells grow freely suspended in the culture medium.
-   [[Embryonic Cell Cultures]]: Cultures derived from embryonic tissues, often pluripotent (e.g., [[Embryonic Stem Cells (ESCs)]]).

Each type has distinct characteristics, advantages, limitations, and applications, influencing the choice of culture system and maintenance techniques.
