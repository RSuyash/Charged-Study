# 2.1.2 Defined Media

Defined media, also known as synthetic media or sometimes serum-free media, represent a significant advancement in [[Animal Cell Culture]]. They are characterized by a precisely known and chemically defined composition, offering greater control, reproducibility, and reduced batch-to-batch variability compared to [[2.1.1 Natural Media]] or serum-supplemented media.

Defined media can be broadly categorized into:
-   [[2.1.2.1 Serum-Dependent Media]]
-   [[2.1.2.2 Serum-Independent Media]] (Serum-Free Media - SFM)
-   [[2.1.2.3 Cell-Specific Media]]

The development of defined media allows for more controlled experimental conditions and is crucial for applications like biopharmaceutical production where consistency and safety are paramount.
