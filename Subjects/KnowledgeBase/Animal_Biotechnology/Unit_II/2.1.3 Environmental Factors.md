# 2.1.3 Environmental Factors

In addition to nutritional requirements provided by [[2.1.1 Natural Media|media]], animal cells in [[Animal Cell Culture]] are highly sensitive to their physical environment. Maintaining optimal environmental conditions is crucial for cell survival, growth, proliferation, and function.

Key environmental factors include:

-   [[2.1.3.1 pH and Buffering Systems]]
-   [[2.1.3.2 CO2 and Bicarbonate Regulation]]
-   [[2.1.3.3 O2 Tension and Requirements]]
-   [[2.1.3.4 Temperature Control]]
-   [[2.1.3.5 Role of Ascorbic Acid and Sugars]] (While technically nutritional, often discussed with environmental controls)

These factors are typically controlled using specialized equipment like [[1.2.2 CO2 Incubator|CO2 incubators]] and bioreactors.
