# 3.3 Biology and Characterization of Cultured Cells

Understanding the biology and performing thorough characterization of cultured cells are essential for ensuring the reliability, reproducibility, and relevance of [[Animal Cell Culture]] experiments and applications. This involves assessing various aspects of cell identity, function, and behavior.

Key aspects include:

-   [[3.3.1 Tissue Typing and Cell-Cell Interaction]]
    -   [[HLA Typing and Tissue Compatibility]]
    -   [[Cell Adhesion Molecules (CAMs) and Cell-Cell Interaction]]
-   [[3.3.2 Scale-up of Cell Culture]]
    -   [[3.3.2.1 Methods for Large-Scale Cell Culture]]
    -   [[3.3.2.2 Measuring Parameters of Growth]]
    -   [[3.3.2.3 Measurement of Cell Death and Viability Assays]]

Characterization helps verify cell line identity, detect contamination or misidentification, monitor genetic stability, assess functional properties, and ensure consistency across experiments and batches.
