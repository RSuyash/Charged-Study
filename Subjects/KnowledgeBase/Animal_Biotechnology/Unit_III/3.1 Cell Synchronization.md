# 3.1 Cell Synchronization

**Cell synchronization** is a technique used in [[Animal Cell Culture]] to bring a population of cells into the same stage of the cell cycle (G1, S, G2, M). In asynchronous cultures, cells are randomly distributed across phases. Synchronization allows for the study of specific cell cycle phases and phase-dependent events in a controlled, uniform manner.
