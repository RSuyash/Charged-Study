# 3.2 Cryopreservation

**Cryopreservation** is a technique for preserving cells, tissues, organs, or other biological materials by cooling them to very low temperatures (typically -80°C or -196°C in [[LN2]]) to halt biological and chemical activity. This allows for long-term storage of viable materials and is critical in [[Animal Cell Culture]], biotechnology, and biomedicine for preserving valuable cell lines, tissues, and genetic resources.
