# 3.3.2 Scale-up of Cell Culture

Scale-up of [[Animal Cell Culture]] is essential for producing sufficient quantities of cells or cell-derived products (e.g., biopharmaceuticals, cell therapies) for research, clinical, and commercial use. Moving from small-scale lab cultures (flasks, dishes) to large-scale [[Bioreactors]] involves significant engineering and biological challenges.

Key aspects include:

-   [[3.3.2.1 Methods for Large-Scale Cell Culture]]
-   [[3.3.2.2 Measuring Parameters of Growth]]
-   [[3.3.2.3 Measurement of Cell Death and Viability Assays]]

The goal is to increase production volume while maintaining cell viability, product quality, and process consistency.
