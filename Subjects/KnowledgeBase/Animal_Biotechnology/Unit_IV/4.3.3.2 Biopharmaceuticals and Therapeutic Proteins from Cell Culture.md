# 4.3.3.2 Biopharmaceuticals and Therapeutic Proteins from Cell Culture

Biopharmaceuticals are protein-based drugs produced using biotechnology, primarily through [[4.3.3.1 Recombinant Protein Production in Cell Culture|recombinant protein production]] in engineered cell cultures. [[Animal Cell Culture]], especially mammalian cell culture ([[CHO Cells]]), is the dominant platform for many complex therapeutic proteins due to its ability to perform necessary post-translational modifications like glycosylation.

## Major Classes of Biopharmaceuticals from Cell Culture

-   **[[Monoclonal Antibodies (mAbs)]]:** Largest class. Used for targeted cancer therapy, autoimmune diseases, inflammation. Produced mainly in [[CHO Cells]]. Examples: Trastuzumab (Herceptin), Rituximab (Rituxan), Adalimumab (Humira), Pembrolizumab (Keytruda).
-   **Recombinant Insulin/Analogs:** Treat diabetes. Produced in [[Yeast]] (*S. cerevisiae*) or [[E. coli]].
-   **Erythropoietin (EPO):** Treats anemia. Produced in [[CHO Cells]].
-   **Growth Factors:** Treat growth disorders (hGH), heart attacks/strokes (tPA). Produced in mammalian cells ([[CHO Cells]]).
-   **Therapeutic Enzymes:** Enzyme replacement therapy for genetic disorders (Imiglucerase for Gaucher, Laronidase for MPS I). Produced in [[CHO Cells]] or other mammalian lines.
-   **[[Vaccine Production Using Cell Culture|Vaccines]]:** Subunit vaccines, [[Viral Vectors for Gene Delivery|viral vector vaccines]], [[Virus-Like Particle (VLP) Vaccines|VLPs]]. Produced in various cell systems (mammalian, insect, yeast).
-   **Cytokines/Interferons:** Treat viral infections, multiple sclerosis (Interferon-alpha, -beta). Produced in [[E. coli]] or mammalian cells.
-   **Fusion Proteins:** Combine domains for enhanced properties (Etanercept - TNF-α inhibitor). Produced in [[CHO Cells]].

Engineered cell culture has revolutionized the pharmaceutical industry, enabling production of complex protein therapeutics that have transformed disease treatment. Advancements continue to improve efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
