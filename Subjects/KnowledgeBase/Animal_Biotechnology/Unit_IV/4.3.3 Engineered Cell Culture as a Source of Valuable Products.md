# 4.3.3 Engineered Cell Culture as a Source of Valuable Products

Engineered [[Animal Cell Culture]] (and microbial culture) serves as a major platform for producing valuable products, offering advantages like improved quality, consistency, scalability, and the ability to produce complex biomolecules difficult to synthesize chemically or extract naturally.

Key product categories include:

-   [[4.3.3.1 Recombinant Protein Production in Cell Culture]]
-   [[4.3.3.2 Biopharmaceuticals and Therapeutic Proteins from Cell Culture]]

This technology underpins much of the modern [[Pharmaceutical Biotechnology]] and [[Industrial Biotechnology]] sectors.
