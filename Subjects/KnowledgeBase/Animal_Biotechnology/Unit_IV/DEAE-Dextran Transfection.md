# DEAE-Dextran Transfection

DEAE-Dextran transfection is a [[4.1.2 Chemical Methods of Cell Transformation|chemical method]] used to introduce DNA into mammalian cells, primarily for transient [[Transfection]]. It utilizes the cationic polymer DEAE-Dextran (diethylaminoethyl-dextran) to facilitate DNA uptake.

*Fig. Putative delivery pathways for DEAE-dextran-DNA complex*

## Mechanism

1.  **Complex Formation:** Positively charged DEAE-Dextran interacts electrostatically with negatively charged DNA, forming large, positively charged DEAE-Dextran-DNA complexes.
2.  **Binding to Cell Surface:** Positively charged complexes bind to the negatively charged cell membrane, increasing local DNA concentration.
3.  **DNA Uptake via Endocytosis (Pinocytosis):** Cells internalize complexes via endocytosis (likely pinocytosis).
4.  **Endosomal Escape & Nuclear Entry (Inefficient):** DNA must escape endosomes to avoid degradation and reach the nucleus. Efficiency is generally low.
5.  **Transient Transfection:** Primarily results in transient expression as DNA usually remains episomal. Stable transformation frequency is very low.

## Procedure

1.  **Cell Prep:** Culture healthy, actively growing mammalian cells.
2.  **Complex Prep:** Dilute DNA in buffer → Mix with DEAE-Dextran solution → Incubate briefly (20-30 min) to form complexes.
3.  **Add Complexes to Cells:** Replace culture medium with complex suspension.
4.  **Incubation:** Incubate cells with complexes (2-6 hours) in a [[1.2.2 CO2 Incubator|CO₂ incubator]].
5.  **Optional Chloroquine Treatment:** Chloroquine (lysosomotropic agent) sometimes added to potentially enhance efficiency (inhibits lysosomal degradation or aids escape), but can be toxic.
6.  **Media Change/Recovery:** Remove complex-containing medium (and chloroquine), wash cells, add fresh medium, incubate for recovery/expression.
7.  **Screening:** Screen/assay cells for transient gene expression or phenotype.

## Applications

-   Transient transfection of mammalian cells (especially COS-7, Vero).
-   Rapid gene expression assays, reporter gene assays.
-   Vaccine production (historically for transient antigen expression).

## Advantages

-   Relatively simple and inexpensive.
-   Effective for transient transfection of certain cell lines.
-   Can co-transfect multiple plasmids.

## Limitations

-   Primarily transient; very low stable transformation efficiency.
-   Cell-type dependent efficiency; not efficient for all lines.
-   DEAE-Dextran can be cytotoxic.
-   Chloroquine (if used) adds toxicity.
-   Sensitive to cell density, DNA quality, parameters; requires optimization.
-   *In vitro* use only.
