# 4.2.2.2 Applications of Hybridoma Technology

[[4.2.2 Hybridoma Technology|Hybridoma technology]], enabling the production of [[Monoclonal Antibodies (mAbs)]], has diverse and significant applications across diagnostics, therapeutics, and research.

![Fig. Applications of hybridoma Technology](<../../../V2/Animal Biotechnology/AB-Unit-IV/Unit IV 1a53a995df87806c8040f81a42fcedc5/image 10.png>)
*Fig. Applications of Hybridoma Technology*

## 1. Diagnostics

-   **In Vitro Diagnostics (IVDs):** mAbs are crucial reagents.
    -   *[[ELISA]] (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay):* Used as capture/detection antibodies for quantifying antigens (proteins, hormones, pathogens, biomarkers) in biological samples (disease diagnosis, pregnancy tests, hormone assays).
    *   *Immunohistochemistry (IHC) / Immunofluorescence (IF):* Stain tissue sections/cells to detect specific antigens/biomarkers (cancer diagnostics, histopathology, research).
    *   *[[Flow Cytometry]]:* Fluorescently labeled mAbs identify/quantify specific cell populations or surface markers (hematology, immunology, cancer diagnostics).
    *   *Lateral Flow Assays (Rapid Tests):* Used in rapid diagnostic tests (pregnancy, infectious diseases).
-   **In Vivo Diagnostics (Imaging):** Radiolabeled or fluorescently labeled mAbs used for imaging tumors, inflammation sites (immunoscintigraphy, radioimmunoguided surgery).

## 2. Therapeutics

mAbs are a major class of drugs, especially for cancer, autoimmune diseases, and inflammatory disorders.

-   **Targeted Cancer Therapy:**
    *   *Blocking Growth Factor Receptors:* (e.g., Trastuzumab/Herceptin for HER2, Cetuximab/Erbitux for EGFR).
    *   *Inducing ADCC/CDC:* Recruit immune cells (NK cells) or complement system to kill cancer cells (e.g., Rituximab/Rituxan, Trastuzumab).
    *   *Antiangiogenesis:* Target VEGF to inhibit tumor blood vessel formation (e.g., Bevacizumab/Avastin).
    *   *Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors:* Block inhibitory signals on T cells (PD-1, CTLA-4) to enhance anti-tumor immunity (e.g., Pembrolizumab/Keytruda, Nivolumab/Opdivo).
    *   *Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs):* Deliver potent cytotoxic drugs selectively to cancer cells.
-   **Autoimmune/Inflammatory Diseases:**
    *   *TNF-α Inhibitors:* (e.g., Infliximab/Remicade, Adalimumab/Humira) for rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease.
    *   *Anti-Integrin mAbs:* (e.g., Natalizumab/Tysabri) for multiple sclerosis.
    *   *Anti-IgE mAbs:* (e.g., Omalizumab/Xolair) for severe asthma.
    *   *Anti-IL-12/IL-23 mAbs:* (e.g., Ustekinumab/Stelara) for IBD, psoriasis.
-   **Prevention of Transplant Rejection:**
    *   *Immunosuppressive mAbs:* Target IL-2 receptor on T cells (e.g., Basiliximab/Simulect) to block activation.
-   **Infectious Diseases:**
    *   *Antiviral mAbs:* (e.g., Palivizumab/Synagis for RSV). mAbs developed against HIV, influenza, etc.
    *   *Antibacterial mAbs:* Explored for treating infections, especially antibiotic-resistant bacteria.

## 3. Research and Development

-   **Basic Research Tools:** Indispensable for:
    *   *Antigen Detection/Quantification:* ELISA, Western blotting, IHC, IF.
    *   *Cell Sorting/Isolation:* FACS.
    *   *Immunoprecipitation:* Isolate specific proteins/complexes.
    *   *Immunofluorescence Microscopy:* Visualize protein localization.
    *   *Neutralization/Blocking Assays:* Study protein/pathway function.
-   **Drug Discovery Tools:**
    *   *Target Validation:* Validate drug targets by blocking/modulating function.
    *   *High-Throughput Screening (HTS) Assays:* Used in screening assays.
    *   *Biosensors/Diagnostic Reagents:* Key components for detecting analytes.

The development of humanized/human mAbs and antibody engineering continues to expand their therapeutic potential.
