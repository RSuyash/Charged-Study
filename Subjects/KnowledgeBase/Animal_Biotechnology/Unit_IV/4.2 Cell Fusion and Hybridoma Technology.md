# 4.2 Cell Fusion and Hybridoma Technology

**Cell fusion** (cell hybridization) joins two or more distinct cells to form a single hybrid cell with multiple nuclei (**heterokaryon**) or, less commonly, a single fused nucleus (**synkaryon**). This technique is powerful for creating cells with combined properties.

A major application is [[4.2.2 Hybridoma Technology]], used for producing [[Monoclonal Antibodies (mAbs)]].
