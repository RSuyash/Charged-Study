# 1.3.2 Miscellaneous Items of Materials

Besides major equipment and [[1.3.1 Filters]], a tissue culture lab requires a wide array of miscellaneous small items and consumables for routine [[Animal Cell Culture]] work, [[Aseptic Technique]], and general lab operations.

![Fig. Items and Materials in Animal Tissue Culture Laboratory](<../../../V2/Animal Biotechnology/AB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 19.png>)
*Fig. Items and Materials in Animal Tissue Culture Laboratory*

## Essential Miscellaneous Items of Materials

1.  **Plasticware (Disposable and Reusable):**
    *   **Tissue Culture Flasks, Dishes, Multiwell Plates:** Sterile, disposable polystyrene, surface-treated for cell attachment (T-flasks, Petri dishes, 6/12/24/96-well plates).
    *   **Centrifuge Tubes (Conical, Bottles):** Sterile, disposable polypropylene/polycarbonate (15 mL, 50 mL, larger bottles) for centrifugation, sample processing, storage.
    *   **Serological Pipettes and Pipette Tips:** (See [[1.2.6 Pipetting Aids]]) Sterile, disposable plastic for aseptic liquid handling.
    *   **Microcentrifuge Tubes (Eppendorf):** Small, disposable polypropylene tubes with snap caps for small volumes.
    *   **Cryovials (Cryogenic Vials):** Specialized plastic vials for [[Cryopreservation]] in [[LN2]]. Cryo-resistant, screw caps with O-rings, labeled for cryogenic storage.
    *   **Petri Dishes (Plastic):** Sterile, disposable for agar plates, colony picking, general use.
    *   **Beakers and Flasks (Plastic, Autoclavable):** Autoclavable polypropylene/polycarbonate for media/solution prep. Lighter, less breakable than glass.

2.  **Glassware (Reusable, Primarily for Prep/Non-Sterile Work):**
    *   **Glass Beakers and Flasks (Borosilicate):** Reusable (Pyrex, Kimax) for media/solution prep, titrations, general chemistry. Autoclavable, chemically resistant.
    *   **Glass Pipettes (Volumetric, Graduated, Pasteur):** Reusable after washing/sterilization. Less common for routine cell culture than disposables.
    *   **Microscope Slides and Coverslips:** For sample prep and viewing under [[1.2.3 Microscopes]].
    *   **Glass Stir Rods and Spatulas:** Reusable for mixing/transferring chemicals.

3.  **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** (See [[1.2.7 Miscellaneous Small Items of Equipments]]) Sterile gloves, lab coats, face masks, eye protection. Essential for safety and aseptic technique.

4.  **Aseptic Technique Supplies:**
    *   **Sterile Swabs and Wipes:** Pre-soaked in 70% ethanol or disinfectant for surface disinfection.
    *   **70% Ethanol or Isopropyl Alcohol:** Essential disinfectant for surfaces, equipment, gloved hands.
    *   **Sterile Water (WFI or Tissue Culture Grade):** For rinsing, media prep, etc.
    *   **Autoclave Tape and Indicator Strips:** Verify sterilization in autoclaves. Tape changes color at temp; strips change color with steam conditions (more reliable).

5.  **Cell Culture Media and Reagents:**
    *   **Basal Media (DMEM, MEM, RPMI 1640, etc.):** Often purchased pre-made, sterile. See [[2.1.1 Natural Media]], [[2.1.2 Defined Media]].
    *   **Serum (FBS, Horse Serum, etc.):** Critical supplement (growth factors, hormones, attachment factors). High-quality, certified, heat-inactivated. See [[2.1.2.1 Serum-Dependent Media]].
    *   **Supplements (Amino Acids, Vitamins, Growth Factors, Hormones):** Added to basal media for complete media.
    *   **Antibiotics and Antimycotics (Penicillin-Streptomycin, Gentamicin, Amphotericin B):** Often added to prevent contamination, but routine use discouraged (masks contamination, selects resistance). See [[2.1 Types of Antibiotics]].
    *   **Buffers (HEPES, Sodium Bicarbonate):** Maintain stable pH. Bicarbonate requires [[CO2 Control]]. HEPES provides additional buffering. See [[2.1.3.1 pH and Buffering Systems]].
    *   **Cell Dissociation Reagents (Trypsin-EDTA, TrypLE, Accutase):** Enzymes/reagents to detach adherent cells.
    *   **Cryopreservation Media (Freezing Media):** Contain cryoprotectants (DMSO, glycerol) for freezing cells. See [[3.2 Cryopreservation]].

6.  **Chemicals and Reagents (General Lab Chemicals):**
    *   **Sterile Water:** (See above).
    *   **Salts, Acids, Bases, Buffers:** For preparing solutions.
    *   **Stains and Dyes (Trypan Blue, Crystal Violet, Giemsa):** For viability assays, cell staining. See [[2.2.1.2 Viable Cell Count Methods]].
    *   **Disinfectants and Cleaning Solutions:** Ethanol, isopropyl alcohol, bleach, quaternary ammonium compounds.

7.  **Consumables for Aseptic Technique:**
    *   **Sterile Gloves:** Latex, nitrile, synthetic, powder-free.
    *   **Sterile Pipettes and Tips:** (See above).
    *   **Sterile Wrappers/Boxes:** For pipettes/tips.
    *   **Sterile Cotton Swabs/Applicators:** For surface disinfection, sampling.
    *   **Sterile Gauze and Wipes:** For cleaning spills.

8.  **Labels and Markers:** (See [[1.2.7 Miscellaneous Small Items of Equipments]]) Permanent and cryogenic.

9.  **Record Keeping and Documentation Supplies:**
    *   **Laboratory Notebooks:** Bound, pre-numbered pages for documenting experiments (GMP/GLP compliance).
    *   **Logbooks:** For equipment usage, maintenance, calibration.
    *   **Data Sheets and Forms:** Standardized data collection.

10. **Safety Supplies:**
    *   **Eye Wash Station and Safety Shower:** Emergency use.
    *   **Fire Extinguisher and Fire Blanket:** Fire safety.
    *   **Spill Kits (Biohazard, Chemical):** For safe cleanup.
    *   **First Aid Kit:** Minor injuries.

Proper planning, budgeting, and inventory management of these miscellaneous materials are necessary for a well-equipped and efficiently operating cell culture facility.
