# 3.1 Cells and Tissue Types: Behavior of cells in culture

[[Animal Cell Culture]] involves growing cells *in vitro*. These cells exhibit diverse behaviors and characteristics influenced by factors like origin, genetics, culture conditions, and adaptation to *in vitro* growth. Understanding these behaviors is key to classifying different types of cell lines used in research and biotechnology.

The main classifications discussed are:
-   [[3.1.1 Primary Cell Lines]]
-   [[3.1.2 Permanent Established Cell Lines]]
-   [[3.1.3 Transformed Cell Lines]]
