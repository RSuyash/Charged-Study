# 1.2.1 Laminar Flow Hoods

Laminar flow hoods, also known as cell culture hoods or clean benches, are essential equipment in a [[1.1 Design of Tissue Culture Laboratory]] designed to provide a localized sterile workspace.

## Principle

They operate on the principle of **laminar airflow**, where air is filtered (typically via [[HEPA Filtration]]) and blown in a unidirectional, smooth, and parallel flow across the work surface. This minimizes turbulence and prevents airborne contaminants from entering the work area, crucial for [[Animal Cell Culture]] manipulations.

## Types of Laminar Flow Hoods

1.  **Horizontal Laminar Flow Hoods:**
    *   **Airflow Direction:** Air drawn from room → pre-filter → HEPA filter → blown horizontally across work surface towards operator.
    *   **Protection:** Primarily *product protection*. Sweeps particles away from the work area.
    *   **Suitable for:** Sterile product handling, media prep, aseptic assembly, non-hazardous cell culture.
    *   **Limitations:** Limited *operator protection* as airflow is towards the operator. Not suitable for hazardous materials or pathogens.

2.  **Vertical Laminar Flow Hoods ([[Biological Safety Cabinets]] - BSCs):**
    *   **Airflow Direction:** Air drawn from top/room → pre-filter → HEPA filter → blown vertically downwards onto work surface. Air is often partially recirculated and partially exhausted (often through another HEPA filter).
    *   **Protection:** Provides *product protection*, *operator protection*, and *environmental protection* (depending on BSC class). Vertical airflow creates an air barrier.
    *   **Types of Vertical Hoods/BSCs:**
        *   **Class II BSCs:** Most common for cell culture (Biosafety Levels 1, 2, 3).
            *   *Class II Type A2:* Most common BSC. Offers product, personnel, environmental protection. Recirculates some air, exhausts HEPA-filtered air into lab or outside.
            *   *Class II Type B2:* Highest Class II containment. Total exhaust (no recirculation). Used for hazardous materials, volatile chemicals, radionuclides.
        *   **Class III BSCs (Glove Boxes):** Gas-tight enclosures for highest containment (Biosafety Level 4). Operator access via glove ports.
    *   **Suitable for:** All aseptic cell culture work, including potentially biohazardous materials. Essential for safety and sterility.

## Key Features and Considerations

-   **Type (Horizontal vs. Vertical/BSC):** Choose based on work type and protection level. Vertical/BSCs recommended for animal cell culture. Class II Type A2 suitable for most applications.
-   **Clean Air Class:** Ensure ISO Class 5 (Class 100) or better air quality.
-   **HEPA Filter Efficiency:** Certified to remove ≥ 99.97% of 0.3 µm particles.
-   **Airflow Velocity/Uniformity:** Check for proper laminar flow (typically 0.3-0.5 m/s).
-   **Work Surface:** Appropriate size, stainless steel or similar material (non-porous, resistant, easy to clean).
-   **Lighting and Ergonomics:** Adequate lighting, comfortable design.
-   **UV Sterilization Lamp (Optional):** For surface sterilization between uses (use with safety precautions).
-   **Electrical Outlets/Gas Valves (Optional):** For equipment/gas lines inside the hood.
-   **Noise Level:** Consider noise from the fan.
-   **Certifications:** Ensure compliance with standards (e.g., NSF/ANSI 49 for BSCs).
-   **Installation and Maintenance:** Proper installation, ducting (if needed), regular maintenance (HEPA filter replacement, certification).

Laminar flow hoods are critical for maintaining aseptic conditions, protecting cultures from contamination, and ensuring reliable cell culture experiments. Vertical laminar flow hoods/BSCs are preferred for animal cell culture due to comprehensive protection.
