# 1.2.7 Miscellaneous Small Items of Equipments

In addition to major equipment, a tissue culture laboratory requires various miscellaneous small items essential for routine [[Animal Cell Culture]] procedures, [[Aseptic Technique]], and general operations. These items are critical for the lab's smooth functioning.

## Essential Miscellaneous Small Items

1.  **Pipettes and Tips:** (See [[1.2.6 Pipetting Aids]])
    *   Micropipettes (adjustable volume, multichannel, electronic) and tips for small volumes.
    *   Serological and Pasteur pipettes for larger volumes.
        ![Fig. Pipette](<../../../V2/Animal Biotechnology/AB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 15.png>)
        *Fig. Pipette*

2.  **Cell Scrapers and Cell Lifters:**
    *   **Purpose:** Detach adherent cells from culture vessels for passaging, harvesting, or assays.
    *   **Types:**
        *   *Cell Scrapers:* Long handle, broad blade (rubber/plastic) for larger surfaces (flasks).
        *   *Cell Lifters:* Shorter handle, angled spatula tip (plastic) for smaller surfaces (dishes, plates).
    *   **Materials:** Sterile, disposable plastic common for single use.
    *   **Usage:** Gentle detachment without damaging cells.

3.  **Cell Strainers (Cell Sieves):**
    *   **Purpose:** Filter cell suspensions to remove clumps/debris, creating single-cell suspensions for counting, flow cytometry, etc.
    *   **Design:** Disposable, sterile plastic devices with mesh filters (nylon) of defined pore sizes (e.g., 40 µm, 70 µm, 100 µm).
    *   **Usage:** Pass cell suspension through by gravity or gentle pressure.

4.  **Hemocytometer or Cell Counter:**
    *   **Purpose:** Manual or automated cell counting for concentration and viability.
    *   **Hemocytometer:** Specialized microscope slide with engraved grid for manual counting. Requires coverslip. Cell concentration calculated based on count, dilution, and chamber volume.
    *   **Automated Cell Counters:**
        *   *Electronic (Coulter Counters):* Use electrical impedance changes. Provide counts and size.
        *   *Image-Based:* Use image analysis software and dyes (e.g., Trypan Blue) for automated counts, viability, and morphology.

5.  **Timers and Stopwatches:**
    *   **Purpose:** Accurate timing for procedures, incubations, reactions.
    *   **Types:** Digital, electronic timers, stopwatches. Should be accurate, easy to use, with alarms.

6.  **Pipette Controllers and Bulbs:** (See [[1.2.6 Pipetting Aids]]) Used with serological/Pasteur pipettes.

7.  **Forceps and Scissors (Surgical Instruments):**
    *   **Purpose:** Fine stainless steel instruments for tissue dissection, sample handling, sterile manipulations.
    *   **Types:** Straight/curved forceps, fine-tipped/dissecting scissors.
    *   **Sterilization:** Must be sterilized (autoclave, dry heat).

8.  **Spatulas and Scoops:**
    *   **Purpose:** Transferring powders, crystals, semi-solids (media components, chemicals).
    *   **Types:** Stainless steel spatulas, plastic scoops.
    *   **Sterilization:** Metal spatulas (autoclave/flaming), disposable plastic scoops (sterile, single-use).

9.  **Wash Bottles and Squeeze Bottles:**
    *   **Purpose:** Dispensing sterile water, ethanol, disinfectants for rinsing/washing.
    *   **Materials:** Autoclavable plastic.
    *   **Contents:** Sterile water, 70% ethanol, disinfectants.

10. **Markers and Labels:**
    *   **Purpose:** Essential for clearly labeling flasks, dishes, tubes, cryovials (cell line, passage #, date, etc.).
    *   **Types:**
        *   *Permanent Markers:* Fine-tipped, waterproof, chemical-resistant.
        *   *Cryogenic Markers/Labels:* Withstand cryogenic temperatures (-196°C) for [[LN2]] storage.

11. **Dispensers for Media and Reagents (Optional):**
    *   **Repeated Pipettes (Steppers):** Repetitive dispensing of fixed volumes.
    *   **Peristaltic Pumps/Dispensers:** Dispensing larger volumes, continuous delivery (perfusion).
    *   **Electronic Dispensers/Burettes:** Precise dispensing for reagent prep/titrations.

12. **Waste Containers and Sharps Containers:**
    *   **Biohazard Waste Containers:** Red bags/rigid containers for contaminated waste (flasks, pipettes, gloves). Autoclave before disposal.
    *   **Sharps Containers:** Puncture-resistant containers for needles, blades, glass pipettes. Prevent injuries.
    *   **Chemical Waste Containers:** Designated containers for chemical waste, follow safety regulations.

13. **Incubator Shakers and Orbital Shakers (Optional, for Suspension Culture):**
    *   **Purpose:** Provide continuous agitation/mixing for suspension cultures (flasks, spinner flasks) ensuring aeration, nutrient distribution, preventing settling.
    *   **Types:** Benchtop incubator shakers, orbital shakers placed inside [[1.2.2 CO2 Incubator]].
    *   **Control:** Speed (RPM), temperature (if incubator shaker).

14. **Water Baths and Dry Baths:**
    *   **Purpose:** Heating samples, thawing cryopreserved cells, maintaining constant temperatures.
    *   **Water Baths:** Water-filled, temperature-controlled. Use sterile water, clean regularly.
    *   **Dry Baths (Heat Blocks):** Electrically heated blocks with wells for tubes/plates. Dry conditions.

15. **pH Meter and Conductivity Meter:**
    *   **Purpose:** Accurately measure/adjust pH of media/buffers. Measure conductivity (salt concentration, water purity).
    *   **Calibration/Maintenance:** Require regular calibration and proper electrode maintenance.

16. **Balances (Analytical, Top-Loading):**
    *   **Purpose:** Accurately weighing chemicals, media components.
    *   **Types:** Analytical (high precision, small quantities), Top-loading (lower precision, higher capacity).
    *   **Calibration:** Calibrate regularly for accuracy.

17. **Vortex Mixers and Magnetic Stirrers:**
    *   **Purpose:** Vortex mixers for rapid mixing of small volumes. Magnetic stirrers for larger volumes, continuous mixing (media prep).
    *   **Magnetic Stirrers with Hot Plates (Optional):** For heating and stirring simultaneously.

18. **Microscope Slides, Coverslips, Counting Chambers (Hemocytometers):** Used for sample prep and viewing/counting under [[1.2.3 Microscopes]].

19. **Timers and Alarms:** (Discussed above) Essential for timing procedures.

20. **Freezer Boxes and Cryo Boxes:**
    *   **Purpose:** Organized storage of cryovials in [[1.2.5 Refrigerators and Freezers]] (-80°C and LN2).
    *   **Types:** Cardboard or plastic, with dividers/racks. Label clearly.

21. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
    *   **Purpose:** Protect personnel from biohazards, chemicals.
    *   **Types:** Sterile gloves (latex, nitrile), lab coats/gowns, face masks, eye protection (safety glasses/goggles), face shields. Wear at all times in the cell culture lab.

Ensuring availability, proper maintenance, and appropriate use of these miscellaneous items is critical for aseptic conditions, accurate procedures, and a safe lab environment.
