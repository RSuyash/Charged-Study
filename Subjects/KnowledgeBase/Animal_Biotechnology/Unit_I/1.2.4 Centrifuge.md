# 1.2.4 Centrifuge

Centrifuges are essential laboratory equipment used to separate components of a mixture based on density by applying centrifugal force. In [[Animal Cell Culture]], they are primarily used for cell harvesting, cell washing, media clarification, and separation of cellular components.

![Fig. Centrifuges in Animal Cell Culture: Types and Key Featrues](<../../../V2/Animal Biotechnology/AB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 9.png>)
*Fig. Centrifuges in Animal Cell Culture: Types and Key Features*

## Principle

Centrifugation separates particles based on size and density differences by spinning samples at high speeds, generating centrifugal force. Denser particles sediment faster than less dense particles.

## Types of Centrifuges Used in Animal Cell Culture

1.  **Benchtop Centrifuges (Laboratory Centrifuges):**
    *   **Design:** Compact, benchtop use. Motor, rotor chamber, rotor, speed/time controls, safety lid. Accommodate various rotors (fixed-angle, swinging-bucket) and tube sizes (microcentrifuge, 15 mL, 50 mL, etc.).
        ![Fig. Centrifuge Parts](<../../../V2/Animal Biotechnology/AB-Unit-I/Unit I 1a53a995df8780f69720e68a76b33240/image 10.png>)
        *Fig. Centrifuge Parts*
    *   **Applications:** Routine cell harvesting/washing, media separation, cell lysate prep, subcellular fraction separation (lower speeds). Essential for general cell culture work.
    *   **Types:**
        *   *Low-Speed Centrifuges (Clinical):* Max speed ~5,000 rpm. Routine cell separation, blood components.
        *   *High-Speed Centrifuges (Refrigerated):* Max speed ≥ 20,000 rpm, refrigerated. Separate smaller particles, organelles, higher efficiency harvesting. Essential for temperature-sensitive samples.
        *   *Microcentrifuges:* Compact, high-speed for small volumes (0.2-2 mL tubes). Max speed ≥ 13,000-15,000 rpm. Rapid pelleting, DNA/RNA precipitation.

2.  **Large-Capacity Refrigerated Centrifuges (Floor-Standing):**
    *   **Design:** Larger, floor-standing, high capacity rotors (liters). Refrigerated. Advanced controls and safety features.
    *   **Applications:** Processing large volumes, large-scale harvesting, clarification of large volumes, pilot-scale bioprocessing.
    *   **Advantages:** Higher capacity, refrigerated, suitable for scale-up.
    *   **Limitations:** Higher cost, require more space, higher energy consumption.

3.  **Ultracentrifuges (Specialized Applications):**
    *   **Design:** Very high speed (>100,000 x g). Specialized, expensive. Separate very small particles (viruses, organelles, proteins, nucleic acids). Require vacuum, sophisticated refrigeration, specialized rotors.
    *   **Types of Ultracentrifugation:**
        *   *Preparative Ultracentrifugation:* Separating/purifying organelles, viruses, lipoproteins, macromolecules.
            *   *Differential Centrifugation:* Sequential steps at increasing g-forces.
            *   *Density Gradient Centrifugation:* Separation based on buoyant density in gradients (sucrose, CsCl). Isopycnic (density) or rate-zonal (size/sedimentation rate).
        *   *Analytical Ultracentrifugation:* Analyzing sedimentation behavior, molecular weights, interactions, homogeneity.
    *   **Applications in Cell Culture:** Specialized research: organelle isolation, virus purification, lipoprotein separation, macromolecule characterization, exosome isolation.
    *   **Advantages:** Extremely high g-forces, high resolution separation.
    *   **Limitations:** Very expensive/complex, specialized rotors/tubes, high energy use, long run times, requires skilled operators, high g-forces/vacuum may affect sample integrity.

## Key Features and Considerations When Selecting a Centrifuge

-   **Maximum Speed (RPM) and G-force:** Select based on application needs (low-speed for harvesting, high-speed for organelles, ultra for specialized).
-   **Rotor Type and Capacity:** Choose compatible rotors (fixed-angle for pelleting, swinging-bucket for gradients) and capacity (number/volume of tubes) for throughput.
-   **Temperature Control (Refrigeration):** Essential for cell culture to maintain low temperatures and prevent sample degradation.
-   **Control System and User Interface:** User-friendly controls, digital displays, programmable settings (speed, time, temp, acceleration/deceleration). Advanced features: protocols, data logging, imbalance detection.
-   **Safety Features:** Crucial features include imbalance detection, overspeed protection, lid interlocks, aerosol containment rotors (for biosafety), rotor recognition.
-   **Benchtop vs. Floor-Standing vs. Ultracentrifuge:** Choose based on scale, volume, g-force needs, and budget.
-   **Maintenance and Service:** Consider requirements, rotor inspection, calibration for reliable and safe operation.

Centrifuges are indispensable for cell separation, harvesting, clarification, and subcellular fractionation in animal cell culture labs.
