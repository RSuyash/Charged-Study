# 1.2.5 Refrigerators and Freezers

Refrigerators and freezers are essential for temperature-controlled storage of various temperature-sensitive materials used in [[Animal Cell Culture]], including reagents, media components, and cell cultures. Proper storage preserves quality, stability, and viability.

## Principle

Refrigerators and freezers maintain specific low temperatures to slow down biological and chemical degradation processes, preserving the integrity of stored materials.

## Types of Refrigerators and Freezers Used in Animal Cell Culture

1.  **Refrigerators (+4°C or 2-8°C, Laboratory Refrigerators):**
    *   **Temperature Range:** Operate above freezing, typically +4°C or 2-8°C.
    *   **Applications:**
        *   Storage of liquid media, reagents (growth factors, supplements, antibiotics).
        *   Storage of reconstituted reagents (short-term).
        *   Short-term storage of prepared media, samples, standards.
    *   **Types:** Laboratory-grade (precise control, forced-air circulation, alarms) or standard household refrigerators.
    *   **Key Features (Lab Grade):** Precise temp control/stability, forced-air circulation, digital display/monitoring, alarms, adjustable shelving, frost-free or manual defrost, optional lockable doors.

2.  **Freezers (-20°C Freezers, Laboratory Freezers):**
    *   **Temperature Range:** Typically operate at -20°C.
    *   **Applications:**
        *   Long-term storage of many reagents, enzymes (growth factors, antibiotics).
        *   Storage of DNA/RNA samples.
        *   Storage of some media components.
    *   **Types:** Laboratory-grade (-20°C, upright or chest) or standard household freezers.
    *   **Key Features (Lab Grade):** Precise temp control/stability, frost-free or low-frost, digital display/monitoring, alarms, adjustable shelving, optional lockable doors.

3.  **Ultra-Low Temperature Freezers (ULT Freezers, -80°C Freezers):**
    *   **Temperature Range:** Typically operate at -80°C. Essential for long-term preservation.
    *   **Applications:**
        *   Long-term [[Cryopreservation]] of cell lines (master/working cell banks).
        *   Storage of cryopreserved samples (cells, tissues, embryos).
        *   Long-term storage of highly labile reagents, enzymes, proteins, antibodies.
        *   RNA storage (prevents degradation).
    *   **Types:** Upright (convenient access) or Chest (better temp stability, longer holdover during power failure).
    *   **Key Features:** Ultra-low temp capability, stability/uniformity, reliability (redundant cooling options), digital display/monitoring/data logging, advanced alarms (remote notification), insulation (VIPs - Vacuum Insulation Panels), optional CO₂/LN₂ backup systems, inventory management systems.

4.  **Liquid Nitrogen (LN₂) Freezers (Cryopreservation Systems, -150°C to -196°C):**
    *   **Temperature Range:** Operate at cryogenic temperatures (-150°C to -196°C) for indefinite preservation.
    *   **Types:**
        *   *Vapor Phase LN₂ Freezers:* Samples stored above liquid nitrogen (-150°C to -190°C). Minimizes cross-contamination risk. Generally preferred for cell/tissue cryopreservation.
        *   *Liquid Phase LN₂ Freezers:* Samples immersed in liquid nitrogen (-196°C). Most stable/coldest, but higher risk of cross-contamination and vial explosion if not sealed properly.
    *   **Applications:**
        *   Long-term cryopreservation (decades/indefinitely) of cell lines (master/working banks, hybridomas, stem cells).
        *   Cryopreservation of tissues, biological specimens for biobanking.
    *   **Key Features:** Ultra-low temp capability, stability/uniformity, LN₂ level monitoring/alarms, temperature monitoring/alarms, optional LN₂ auto-fill systems, vapor phase storage preferred, inventory management systems.

## Key Considerations When Selecting Refrigerators and Freezers

-   **Temperature Requirements:** Match temperature range to materials being stored (+4°C, -20°C, -80°C, -196°C).
-   **Temperature Stability and Uniformity:** Crucial to prevent fluctuations affecting sample quality.
-   **Reliability and Backup Systems:** Choose reliable equipment; consider backups (CO₂/LN₂) for critical samples in ULT freezers.
-   **Capacity and Storage Space:** Select adequate capacity for current/future needs. Consider inventory management.
-   **Alarm and Monitoring Systems:** Essential for alerting users to deviations. Remote monitoring adds security.
-   **Energy Efficiency and Operating Costs:** Important consideration, especially for ULT freezers.
-   **Footprint and Space Requirements:** Choose units that fit available lab space.
-   **Security Features (Optional):** Lockable doors for valuable/sensitive materials.
-   **Certifications and Standards:** Ensure compliance (e.g., UL, CE).

Refrigerators and freezers are critical infrastructure for maintaining the integrity of cell culture materials and experimental reproducibility.
