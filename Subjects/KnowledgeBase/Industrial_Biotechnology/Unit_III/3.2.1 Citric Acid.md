# 3.2.1 Citric Acid

Citric acid is a tricarboxylic acid, an intermediate in the Krebs cycle, and one of the most important organic acids produced by fermentation. It has wide applications in food, beverage, pharmaceutical, and chemical industries.

*Fig. Citric Acid Production*

## 3.2.1.1 Microorganisms Involved in Citric Acid Production

-   ***Aspergillus niger* (Filamentous Fungus):** Predominant industrial producer. High production capacity, GRAS status. Optimized strains are used.
-   **Yeasts (Less Common):**
    -   ***Candida* species (*C. guilliermondii*, *C. lipolytica*):** Can produce citric acid, especially from *n*-alkanes or glycerol.
    -   ***Yarrowia lipolytica*:** Oleaginous yeast, produces citric acid from various substrates.
-   **Bacteria (Less Common):**
    -   ***Bacillus subtilis*:** Some strains produce citric acid, but yields are generally lower than fungal production.

*A. niger* is preferred due to high yields and established technology.

## 3.2.1.2 Fermentation Process for Citric Acid Production

Typically uses submerged fermentation (SmF) with *Aspergillus niger*.

![Fig. Production of Citric Acid](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit III 1a63a995df878010bfefd5d2faf7d80a/image%206.png>)
*Fig. Production of Citric Acid*

**Submerged Fermentation (SmF):**

-   **Raw Materials:**
    -   **Carbon Source:** Carbohydrates (Molasses - beet/cane, Glucose Syrup, Sucrose, Starch hydrolysates). Molasses is cost-effective.
    -   **Nitrogen Source:** Limited amounts to promote citric acid overproduction. Ammonium salts (nitrate, sulfate, chloride).
    -   **Minerals:** Phosphate (KH₂PO₄), Magnesium (MgSO₄), Trace Metals (Fe, Zn, Mn, Cu, Mo). Iron and Manganese concentrations are critical and kept low to favor citric acid accumulation.
    -   **Methanol (Optional):** Small amounts may enhance production in molasses media.
-   **Fermentation Conditions:**
    -   **Type:** Batch or Fed-batch (preferred for high productivity).
    -   **Aeration:** Strictly Aerobic (requires high DO).
    -   **Temperature:** 28-32°C.
    -   **pH:** Critically controlled at low acidic pH (1.5-2.5). Inhibits contaminants, favors extracellular accumulation of free citric acid.
    -   **Agitation:** For mixing, O₂ transfer.
    -   **Inoculum:** *A. niger* spores.
-   **Process Control:** DO, temperature, pH, substrate feeding (fed-batch), nitrogen limitation, trace metal control (low Fe, Mn), foam control, morphology control (pellets vs. dispersed mycelia).

## 3.2.1.3 Product Recovery and Applications of Citric Acid

**Product Recovery (Downstream Processing):**

![Fig. Citric Acid Selective Recovery](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit III 1a63a995df878010bfefd5d2faf7d80a/image%207.png>)
*Fig. Citric Acid Selective Recovery*

1.  **Cell Mass Removal:** Filtration (rotary vacuum, pressure) to remove *A. niger* mycelia.
2.  **Citric Acid Precipitation:** Add Calcium hydroxide (lime) to clarified broth to precipitate insoluble calcium citrate (at elevated temperature). Wash precipitate.
3.  **Citric Acid Regeneration:** Treat calcium citrate with Sulfuric acid to regenerate citric acid and precipitate calcium sulfate (gypsum). Filter off gypsum.
4.  **Purification:** Treat crude citric acid solution with activated carbon (optional) for decolorization.
5.  **Concentration & Crystallization:** Concentrate solution by evaporation, then crystallize citric acid by cooling. Separate crystals by centrifugation and dry.

**Applications:**

-   **Food & Beverage (Largest Use):**
    -   **Acidulant:** Tart taste in drinks, juices.
    -   **Preservative:** Inhibits microbial growth (jams, jellies, candies).
    -   **Flavoring Agent:** Candies, desserts.
    -   **Antioxidant Synergist:** Enhances Vitamin C effectiveness.
-   **Pharmaceutical:**
    -   **Acidulant:** pH adjustment, taste masking.
    -   **Anticoagulant:** Citrate salts for blood collection.
    -   **Effervescent Formulations:** With bicarbonates.
    -   **Stabilizer/Antioxidant.**
-   **Chemical Industry:**
    -   **Chelating Agent:** Binds metal ions (detergents, cleaning, scale/rust removal).
    -   **Builder:** In detergents/soaps.
    -   **Industrial Cleaning:** Equipment, boilers, metal treatment.
    -   **Plasticizer:** Citric acid esters for polymers.
-   **Cosmetics:** pH adjuster, antioxidant.
