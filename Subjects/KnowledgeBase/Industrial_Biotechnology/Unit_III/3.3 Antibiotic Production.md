# 3.3 Antibiotic Production

Antibiotics are microbial secondary metabolites that inhibit or kill other microorganisms, primarily bacteria. They are crucial for treating bacterial infections. Fermentation is the main method for large-scale antibiotic production, such as Penicillin and Erythromycin.
