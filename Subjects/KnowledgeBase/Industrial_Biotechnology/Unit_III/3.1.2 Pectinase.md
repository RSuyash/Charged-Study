# 3.1.2 Pectinase

Pectinases hydrolyze pectin, a polysaccharide in plant cell walls. They are widely used in the food and beverage industry (fruit juice, wine).

## 3.1.2.1 Microorganisms Involved in Pectinase Production

-   **Fungi (Major Producers):**
    -   ***Aspergillus niger*:** Most important industrial producer. High secretion, GRAS status. Produces a complex of pectinolytic enzymes (polygalacturonases, pectin lyases, pectin esterases).
    -   ***Aspergillus awamori*:** Also used.
    -   ***Trichoderma reesei*:** Produces pectinases alongside cellulases.
-   **Bacteria (Less Common Industrially):**
    -   ***Bacillus* species (*B. subtilis*, *B. licheniformis*):** Produce pectin lyases.
    -   ***Erwinia chrysanthemi*:** Produces pectate lyases.

Fungi, especially *A. niger*, are preferred due to high secretion, GRAS status, and enzyme diversity.

## 3.1.2.2 Fermentation Process for Pectinase Production

Uses submerged fermentation (SmF) or solid-state fermentation (SSF). SmF is more common industrially.

**Submerged Fermentation (SmF):**

-   **Raw Materials:**
    -   **Carbon Source/Inducer:** Pectin-rich materials (Citrus Pectin, Apple Pomace, Sugar Beet Pulp, other agricultural residues). Simple sugars (glucose, sucrose) may supplement for initial growth.
    -   **Nitrogen Source:** Organic (Corn Steep Liquor, Yeast Extract). Inorganic (Ammonium salts) can supplement.
    -   **Minerals:** P, Mg, Ca, Fe, Zn, Mn.
-   **Fermentation Conditions:**
    -   **Type:** Batch or Fed-batch.
    -   **Aeration:** Aerobic (required for fungi like *A. niger*).
    -   **Temperature:** Mesophilic range (25-30°C for *A. niger*).
    -   **pH:** Controlled at slightly acidic (pH 4.0-5.5 for *A. niger*). Helps minimize bacterial contamination.
    -   **Agitation:** For mixing, O₂ transfer.
    -   **Induction:** Pectin-rich substrates induce pectinase production.
-   **Process Control:** DO, temperature, pH, nutrient feeding (fed-batch), foam control, SCADA systems.

**Solid-State Fermentation (SSF):**

-   **Substrates:** Pectin-rich solids (Citrus Peels, Apple Pomace, Wheat Bran). Moistened to 50-70%.
-   **Fermentation Conditions:**
    -   **Aeration:** Aerobic (natural or forced).
    -   **Temperature:** Controlled via aeration/heat management.
    -   **Moisture:** Critical parameter.
    -   **pH:** Generally not actively controlled.
    -   **Inoculation:** Spores of fungi (*A. niger*).

## 3.1.2.3 Product Recovery and Applications of Pectinase

**Product Recovery (Downstream Processing):**

1.  **Solid-Liquid Separation:**
    -   **SmF:** Cell removal (centrifugation/filtration).
    -   **SSF:** Enzyme extraction from solid, then solid removal.
2.  **Enzyme Concentration:** Ultrafiltration (UF) or precipitation (less common).
3.  **Purification (Chromatography):** Often used in crude/partially purified form. IEX or SEC for higher purity if needed.
4.  **Formulation and Stabilization:** Liquid concentrates or dried powders with stabilizers.

**Applications:**

-   **Fruit Juice Processing (Largest Application):**
    -   **Clarification:** Reduces viscosity, removes haze (apple, citrus, berry juices).
    -   **Extraction/Yield Improvement:** Increases juice yield from pulp.
    -   **Viscosity Reduction:** Easier processing of purees/concentrates.
-   **Winemaking:**
    -   **Clarification:** Removes haze, improves filterability.
    -   **Color Extraction:** Enhances color release from grape skins (red wine).
    -   **Flavor/Aroma Enhancement:** Releases compounds from cell walls.
-   **Vegetable Processing:** Juice/puree clarification, viscosity reduction, potato processing.
-   **Plant Fiber Processing:** Fiber extraction (flax, hemp), biopolishing of cotton.
-   **Other:** Paper/pulp industry (bio-bleaching), animal feed (improving digestibility).
-   **Research/Assays:** Pectin analysis, plant cell wall studies.
