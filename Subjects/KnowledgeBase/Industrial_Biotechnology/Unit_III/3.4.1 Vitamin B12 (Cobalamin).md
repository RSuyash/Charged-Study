# 3.4.1 Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin)

Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) is a complex, cobalt-containing vitamin essential for DNA synthesis, red blood cell formation, and nerve function. It is produced only by microorganisms. Industrial production relies on fermentation.

## 3.4.1.1 Microorganisms Involved in Vitamin B12 Production

-   ***Pseudomonas denitrificans* (Bacteria):** Predominant modern industrial producer. High yields, facultative anaerobe. Genetically engineered strains used.
-   ***Bacillus megaterium* (Bacteria):** Also used industrially, sometimes in co-fermentation. Gram-positive, spore-forming.
-   ***Propionibacterium* species (*P. freudenreichii*, *P. shermanii*) (Bacteria):** Historically important, produce B12 anaerobically. Less used now due to lower yields/slower growth compared to *P. denitrificans*.

## 3.4.1.2 Fermentation Process for Vitamin B12 Production

Complex, multi-stage process using submerged fermentation (SmF), often a hybrid aerobic/anaerobic approach.

**Hybrid Aerobic/Anaerobic Process Outline:**

1.  **Aerobic Growth Phase (Biomass Production):**
    -   **Microorganism:** *P. denitrificans* or *B. megaterium*.
    -   **Conditions:** Aerobic SmF, optimal pH/temp for growth.
    -   **Nutrients:** Carbon source (glucose, sucrose, molasses), Nitrogen source (corn steep liquor, yeast extract, ammonium salts), Cobalt source (CoCl₂, CoSO₄ - essential precursor), other minerals. Betaine, DMB precursors optional.
    -   **Purpose:** Rapid biomass accumulation.
2.  **Anaerobic Production Phase (B12 Synthesis):**
    -   **Transition:** Reduce/eliminate aeration after sufficient biomass growth.
    -   **Conditions:** Anaerobic or microaerophilic SmF. Optimal pH/temp for synthesis.
    -   **Feeding (Fed-batch):** Controlled feeding of Carbon source, Cobalt source.
    -   **Precursor Feeding:** 5,6-Dimethylbenzimidazole (DMB) or precursors fed during this phase (often limiting).
    -   **Purpose:** Induce and maximize B12 biosynthesis.

**Variations:** Strictly aerobic processes, co-fermentation, two-stage fermentation exist. Genetic engineering enhances yields.

**Process Control:** DO (critical for phase transition), temperature, pH, substrate/cobalt/DMB feeding rates, redox potential (anaerobic phase), foam control.

## 3.4.1.3 Product Recovery and Applications of Vitamin B12

**Product Recovery (Downstream Processing):**

Complex process as B12 is primarily intracellular.

1.  **Cell Mass Removal:** Centrifugation.
2.  **Cell Disruption (Lysis):** Release intracellular B12 (Heat treatment, chemical lysis - alkali/detergent, enzymatic lysis).
3.  **Vitamin B12 Extraction:** From cell lysate using solvent extraction (chloroform, phenol, cresol) or adsorption chromatography (activated carbon, resin).
4.  **Purification (Chromatography):** Further purification using ion-exchange, adsorption chromatography, or preparative HPLC (for pharmaceutical grade).
5.  **Crystallization:** Crystallize B12 from purified solution by adjusting solvent, temperature, pH.
6.  **Drying:** Separate crystals (filtration/centrifugation) and dry (vacuum).

**Applications:**

-   **Nutritional Supplement & Food Fortification (Primary Use):** For vegans/vegetarians, individuals with deficiency/malabsorption. Fortification of cereals, plant milks.
-   **Pharmaceutical:** Treatment of B12 deficiency (megaloblastic anemia, neurological issues), pernicious anemia (injections). Potential use in neurological disorders.
-   **Animal Feed Additive:** Improves growth, health, feed efficiency (poultry, swine).
-   **Diagnostic & Research:** Assays, biochemical studies.
