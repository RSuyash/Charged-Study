# 3.1.1 Protease

Proteases (peptidases, proteinases) catalyze the hydrolysis of peptide bonds in proteins. They are a major class of industrial enzymes.

## 3.1.1.1 Microorganisms Involved in Protease Production

Various microorganisms produce proteases:

-   **Bacteria:**
    -   ***Bacillus* species (*B. subtilis*, *B. licheniformis*, *B. amyloliquefaciens*):** Major producers of alkaline and neutral proteases (e.g., subtilisins) used in detergents, food, textiles. Gram-positive, spore-forming, high secretion.
    -   ***Pseudomonas* species:** Produce proteases, less common industrially than *Bacillus*.
-   **Fungi:**
    -   ***Aspergillus* species (*A. niger*):** Important producers of acid and metalloproteases. *A. niger* has GRAS status and high secretion capacity.
    -   ***Trichoderma reesei*:** Produces proteases alongside cellulases.
-   **Actinomycetes:**
    -   ***Streptomyces* species (*S. griseus*):** Produce extracellular proteases used in leather processing and pharmaceuticals.

Choice depends on desired protease characteristics (pH/temp optimum, specificity), cost, and downstream processing.

## 3.1.1.2 Fermentation Process for Protease Production

Typically uses submerged fermentation (SmF) or solid-state fermentation (SSF).

*Fig. Fermentation Process for Enzyme Production*

**Submerged Fermentation (SmF):**

-   **Raw Materials:**
    -   **Carbon Source:** Carbohydrates (glucose, starch, molasses), oils, glycerol.
    -   **Nitrogen Source:** Often proteinaceous materials (soybean meal, casein, corn steep liquor, yeast extract, peptone) which act as inducers. Inorganic sources (ammonium salts) can supplement.
    -   **Minerals:** P, Mg, Ca, Fe, Zn, Mn.
    -   **Growth Factors:** Vitamins, amino acids if required.
-   **Fermentation Conditions:**
    -   **Type:** Batch or Fed-batch (common for high yields).
    -   **Aeration:** Aerobic (requires sufficient DO).
    -   **Temperature:** Mesophilic range (25-37°C for *Bacillus*, 25-30°C for fungi).
    -   **pH:** Controlled near neutral/alkaline (pH 7.0-8.5 for bacterial alkaline proteases) or acidic/neutral (pH 5.0-7.0 for fungal acid proteases).
    -   **Agitation:** For mixing, O₂ transfer, temperature uniformity.
    -   **Inoculum:** Multi-stage scale-up.
-   **Process Control:** DO, temperature, pH, nutrient feeding (fed-batch), foam control, SCADA systems.

![Fig. Submerged Fermentation (SmF)](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit III 1a63a995df878010bfefd5d2faf7d80a/image%201.png>)
*Fig. Submerged Fermentation (SmF)*

**Solid-State Fermentation (SSF):**

-   **Substrates:** Solid agricultural residues (wheat bran, rice bran, soybean meal) as nutrient source and support. Moistened to 50-70%.
-   **Fermentation Conditions:**
    -   **Aeration:** Aerobic (natural or forced).
    -   **Temperature:** Controlled via aeration/heat management.
    -   **Moisture:** Critical parameter, controlled by initial moistening and aeration.
    -   **pH:** Generally not actively controlled.
    -   **Inoculation:** Spores or mycelia (often fungi like *Aspergillus*).
-   **Process Control:** Moisture, aeration, temperature, substrate properties. Incubation time is longer (days/weeks).

## 3.1.1.3 Product Recovery and Applications of Protease

**Product Recovery (Downstream Processing):**

![Fig. Downstream Process](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit III 1a63a995df878010bfefd5d2faf7d80a/image%203.png>)
*Fig. Downstream Process*

1.  **Cell Mass Removal:** Centrifugation or filtration.
2.  **Product Concentration:** Ultrafiltration (UF) or precipitation (ammonium sulfate, solvent).
3.  **Purification (Chromatography):** Affinity, Ion-Exchange (IEX), Size-Exclusion (SEC) for higher purity.
4.  **Formulation and Stabilization:** Drying (spray drying, freeze drying), addition of stabilizers. Liquid or solid formulations.

**Applications:**

-   **Detergents:** Alkaline proteases (subtilisins) for removing protein stains.
-   **Food Industry:**
    -   **Baking:** Dough improvement (gluten modification).
    -   **Meat Tenderization:** Papain, bromelain, ficin.
    -   **Cheese Making:** Rennin (chymosin) or microbial rennets for milk clotting.
    -   **Brewing:** Haze prevention, clarity improvement.
-   **Leather Processing:** Dehairing, bating (softening).
-   **Textile Industry:** Biopolishing, desizing, bio-scouring.
-   **Pharmaceutical Industry:** Therapeutic enzymes (wound debridement, anti-inflammatory), production of peptide drugs.
-   **Bioremediation:** Degradation of proteinaceous waste.
-   **Research and Diagnostics:** Protein analysis, peptide synthesis, cell lysis.
