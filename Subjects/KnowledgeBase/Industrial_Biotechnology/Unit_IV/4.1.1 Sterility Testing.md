# 4.1.1 Sterility Testing

**Definition and Purpose:**
Sterility testing is a critical QC test to ensure a pharmaceutical or biological product is free from viable microorganisms. It's essential for parenteral drugs, ophthalmic preparations, etc., to prevent infections and ensure patient safety.

![Fig. Sterility Testing](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit IV 1a63a995df87808d87d7e8caf4ceadb4/image%201.png>)
*Fig. Sterility Testing*

**Principles:**
Based on probability, testing a representative sample to detect low levels of contamination.

**Methods (Pharmacopoeial - USP, EP, JP):**

1.  **Membrane Filtration Method:**
    *   **Principle:** Method of choice for filterable products. Sample filtered through a 0.45 µm (or smaller) sterile membrane, retaining microbes. Membrane cultured.
    *   **Procedure:**
        1.  Aseptically filter sample.
        2.  Transfer membrane halves to Tryptic Soy Broth (TSB, for aerobes/fungi) and Fluid Thioglycollate Medium (FTM, for anaerobes/aerobes).
        3.  Incubate (TSB at 20-25°C, FTM at 30-35°C) for at least 14 days.
        4.  Observe for turbidity (microbial growth).
        5.  Interpretation: No turbidity = Pass. Turbidity = Fail.
    *   **Suitability Test:** Required to ensure product doesn't inhibit microbial growth (bacteriostasis/fungistasis).

    ![Fig. Membrane Filtration Unit](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit IV 1a63a995df87808d87d7e8caf4ceadb4/ccd933ae-76c1-4a45-adde-e19cbb02d73d.png>)
    *Fig. Membrane Filtration Unit*

2.  **Direct Inoculation Method (Direct Transfer):**
    *   **Principle:** Used for non-filterable products (ointments, creams, viscous liquids, devices). Product directly inoculated into media.
    *   **Procedure:**
        1.  Aseptically transfer product into TSB and FTM. (May require dispersion/dilution).
        2.  Incubate as above.
        3.  Observe and interpret as above.
    *   **Suitability Test:** Required to ensure product doesn't inhibit growth.

    ![Fig. Direct Inoculation Method](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/Unit IV 1a63a995df87808d87d7e8caf4ceadb4/image%202.png>)
    *Fig. Direct Inoculation Method*

**Media Used:**
-   **Soybean-Casein Digest Medium (Tryptic Soy Broth - TSB):** For aerobic bacteria and fungi.
-   **Fluid Thioglycollate Medium (FTM):** For anaerobic and aerobic bacteria.

**Interpretation:**
-   **Pass:** No growth in any media.
-   **Fail:** Growth in any media. Requires investigation/corrective action.
-   **Invalid:** Issues with procedure (contamination, false positives). May require repeat testing.

**Regulatory Requirements:**
Mandatory test performed according to pharmacopoeial guidelines (USP, EP, JP) and regulatory agencies (FDA, EMA). Critical aspect of Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP).
