# 4.4 Quality Assurance (QA) and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)

Quality Assurance (QA) and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) are fundamental to the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries, ensuring products are consistently produced and controlled to meet quality standards for safety and efficacy. QA focuses on preventing defects through systems, while GMP provides specific guidelines for manufacturing processes.
