# 4.4.2 GMP Principles and Guidelines

**Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)** are internationally recognized guidelines defining minimum quality standards for manufacturing regulated products (pharmaceuticals, biopharmaceuticals, medical devices, food). GMP ensures products are consistently produced and controlled according to quality standards appropriate for their intended use and regulatory requirements.

**Core Principles of GMP (Often summarized as "10 Golden Rules"):**

1.  **Write Procedures and Follow Them (SOPs):**
    *   Critical processes/procedures defined in written Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
    *   Manufacturing/testing must follow SOPs for consistency, error reduction, traceability.

2.  **Follow Written Procedures and Document Work (Documentation):**
    *   All steps, tests, observations documented accurately, legibly, contemporaneously ("If it isn’t documented, it wasn’t done").
    *   Essential for traceability, accountability, regulatory compliance.
    *   Includes: Batch Records, SOPs, Specifications, Test Methods, Equipment Records, Training Records, Deviation Reports, CAPA Records, Change Control Records.

3.  **Validate Processes and Procedures:**
    *   Manufacturing processes and analytical methods validated to demonstrate consistency and reliability.
    *   Provides high assurance of meeting pre-determined acceptance criteria.
    *   Includes: Process Validation, Cleaning Validation, Analytical Method Validation, Equipment Validation (Qualification - IQ/OQ/PQ).

4.  **Design and Use Appropriate Facilities and Equipment:**
    *   Facilities/equipment designed, constructed, located, maintained to ensure quality, prevent contamination, facilitate cleaning/maintenance.
    *   Includes: Facility design (cleanrooms, separation), Equipment design (materials, cleanability), Maintenance/Cleaning programs.

5.  **Control Raw Materials, Components, and Packaging Materials:**
    *   Materials must be of appropriate quality, tested against specifications.
    *   Includes: Supplier Qualification, Material Specifications, Material Testing/Inspection, Proper Handling/Storage, Material Traceability.

6.  **Label and Control Products and Containers:**
    *   Clear, accurate labeling for identification, traceability, preventing mix-ups. Must comply with regulations.
    *   Includes: Accurate labels (product name, batch #, expiry), Label Control/Reconciliation, Batch Numbering, Controlled Storage/Quarantine areas.

7.  **Conduct Laboratory Controls and Testing (Quality Control):**
    *   QC testing monitors quality, process performance, compliance. Performed at appropriate stages (raw materials, in-process, finished product) using validated methods.
    *   Includes: Established QC Lab, Validated Methods, Sampling/Testing Procedures, Data Analysis/Documentation, Specifications/Acceptance Criteria, Batch Release Decisions, Reference Standards.

8.  **Handle Rejections and Recalls:**
    *   System for handling rejected materials/products and managing recalls.
    *   Includes: Quarantine/Identification of rejects, Investigation of deviations/OOS/complaints, Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA), Recall Procedures.

9.  **Conduct Internal Audits and Inspections (Self-Inspection):**
    *   Regular internal audits monitor GMP compliance, identify improvements.
    *   Includes: Scheduled audits (by QAU), defined scope, written reports, management review, corrective actions.

10. **Training and Competence of Personnel:**
    *   All personnel adequately trained/qualified for tasks, understand GMP.
    *   Includes: Training Programs (GMP, SOPs, job-specific), Training Records, Periodic Retraining/Competency Evaluation.

**GMP Guidelines and Regulations:**
Issued by regulatory agencies, often legally binding.
-   **US FDA GMP:** 21 CFR Parts 210, 211.
-   **EU GMP:** EudraLex Volume 4.
-   **WHO GMP:** International guidelines.
-   **ICH Guidelines:** e.g., ICH Q7 (GMP for APIs).

Compliance is essential for product quality, safety, efficacy, and regulatory approval. GMP is a continuous culture of quality.
