# 4.2.4 Operating Cost Estimates

Operating costs are the ongoing, recurring expenses for day-to-day plant operation and production. Accurate estimates are crucial for profitability, optimization, pricing, and financial planning.

**Categories of Operating Costs (Detailed):**

1.  **Raw Materials Costs (Feedstock):**
    *   *Carbon Source:* Type (glucose, molasses, etc.), purity, unit cost, consumption rate.
    *   *Nitrogen Source:* Type (ammonium salts, yeast extract, etc.), purity, unit cost, consumption rate.
    *   *Other Nutrients:* Minerals, vitamins, growth factors, buffers, antifoams. Unit costs and consumption rates.
    *   *Media Preparation Costs:* Labor, equipment, energy for mixing, sterilization.

2.  **Utilities Costs:**
    *   *Electricity:* For fermenter (agitation, aeration, cooling), DSP equipment, utilities (boilers, chillers, HVAC). Based on power consumption and electricity rate (€/kWh).
    *   *Steam:* For sterilization, heating. Based on consumption rate and generation costs (fuel, water treatment).
    *   *Cooling Water:* For fermenter/DSP cooling. Based on consumption rate and treatment costs.
    *   *Water:* Raw water purchase and treatment costs (process water, purified water, WFI). Based on consumption rate.
    *   *Compressed Air:* For aeration. Based on consumption rate and generation costs (compressor electricity/maintenance).

3.  **Labor Costs:**
    *   *Direct Production Labor:* Fermentation & DSP operators, supervisors. Based on labor rate (€/hr) and hours per batch/unit.
    *   *QC/QA Labor:* Analysts, technicians, QA staff, regulatory personnel.
    *   *Maintenance & Engineering Labor:* Technicians, engineers for upkeep.

4.  **Consumables & Supplies Costs:**
    *   *Filters & Membranes:* Depth/micro/ultrafilters, RO membranes. Unit costs, consumption rates, replacement frequency.
    *   *Chromatography Resins & Columns:* Resin cost (€/L), column cost, lifespan, regeneration.
    *   *Chemicals & Reagents:* For cell disruption, precipitation, extraction, mobile phases, cleaning, sanitization, QC testing. Unit costs and consumption rates.
    *   *Glassware & Plasticware:* Lab disposables, PPE, cleanroom supplies, final product packaging (vials, labels).

5.  **Waste Disposal Costs:**
    *   *Biomass Waste:* Sludge dewatering, transport, landfill/incineration fees.
    *   *Spent Media/Liquid Waste:* Wastewater treatment costs (biological, chemical), sludge disposal.
    *   *Solvent Waste:* Disposal or recovery/recycling costs.
    *   *Hazardous Waste:* Handling, treatment, disposal according to regulations.

6.  **Maintenance & Repair Costs (Annualized):**
    *   Preventative maintenance, repairs, spare parts inventory, maintenance labor. Often estimated as % of equipment cost/year (e.g., 2-5%).

7.  **Overhead Costs (Fixed Operating Costs):**
    *   *Plant Overhead:* Rent/depreciation (buildings), property taxes, insurance, base utilities, security, admin overhead.
    *   *QA/Regulatory Compliance:* QA department costs, regulatory affairs, validation costs (annualized), GMP compliance costs.

**Methods for Operating Cost Estimation:**
-   **Detailed Cost Analysis:** Based on material quantities, consumption rates, unit costs, labor hours.
-   **Vendor Quotes & Supplier Pricing:** For raw materials, chemicals, consumables, utilities.
-   **Historical Data & Benchmarking:** Using data from similar processes or industry standards.
-   **Process Simulation & Modeling:** Estimating utility/material consumption, waste generation.
-   **Spreadsheet-Based Cost Models:** Organizing data, performing calculations, analyzing parameter impacts.

Accurate operating cost estimates are vital for economic feasibility, profitability, and competitiveness. They require detailed analysis and are often combined with capital cost estimates for a full economic evaluation.
