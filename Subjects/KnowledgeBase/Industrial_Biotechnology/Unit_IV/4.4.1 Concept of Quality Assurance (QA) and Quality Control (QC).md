# 4.4.1 Concept of Quality Assurance (QA) and Quality Control (QC)

QA and QC are interrelated but distinct concepts within a quality management system, both essential for ensuring product quality.

**Quality Assurance (QA):**

-   **Definition:** A broad system of planned activities to provide confidence that quality requirements **will be** fulfilled.
-   **Focus:** **Process-oriented**, focuses on **preventing** defects ("Building Quality In"). Proactive.
-   **Scope:** Encompasses the entire quality system:
    -   Quality Management System (QMS) establishment.
    -   Documentation Control (SOPs, specs, records).
    -   Training & Personnel Qualification.
    -   Supplier Quality Management.
    -   Process Validation.
    -   Equipment Qualification & Calibration.
    -   Internal Audits.
    -   Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA).
    -   Change Control.
    -   Management Review.
-   **Orientation:** System-oriented, management function.

**Quality Control (QC):**

-   **Definition:** Specific activities and tests to **verify** and **measure** product/process quality against standards.
-   **Focus:** **Product-oriented**, focuses on **detecting** defects ("Testing Quality In"). Reactive.
-   **Scope:** Primarily testing and inspection:
    -   Raw Material Testing.
    -   In-Process Quality Control (IPQC).
    -   Finished Product Testing (identity, purity, potency, sterility, etc.).
    -   Using Validated Analytical Methods.
    -   Inspection & Sampling.
    -   Data Analysis & Documentation (comparing results to specs).
    -   Release/Rejection Decisions.
    -   Stability Testing.
-   **Orientation:** Product-oriented, technical function.

**Relationship:**

-   QA sets the system (prevention), QC checks the output (detection/verification).
-   QA is proactive, QC is reactive.
-   QA is broader (management), QC is specific (technical testing).
-   Both are essential for overall quality.

**Analogy (Building a House):**
-   **QA:** Building plan, quality materials, proper construction techniques, checks during building (process-focused, prevention).
-   **QC:** Final inspection of the completed house against specifications (product-focused, detection).
