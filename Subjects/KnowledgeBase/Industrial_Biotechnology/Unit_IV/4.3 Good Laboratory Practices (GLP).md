# 4.3 Good Laboratory Practices (GLP)

Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) are principles ensuring the quality, reliability, and integrity of non-clinical laboratory safety studies supporting regulated products (pharmaceuticals, pesticides, etc.). GLP covers study design, conduct, data recording, reporting, and archiving.
