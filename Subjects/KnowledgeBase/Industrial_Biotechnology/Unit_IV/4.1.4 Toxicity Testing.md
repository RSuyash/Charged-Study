# 4.1.4 Toxicity Testing

**Definition and Purpose:**
Toxicity testing (toxicological/safety testing) evaluates potential adverse effects of substances (drugs, chemicals, pollutants) on living organisms using *in vitro*, *in vivo*, and *in silico* assays. It's crucial for preclinical safety assessment before human/environmental exposure.
Purpose:
-   Identify potential hazards.
-   Characterize adverse effects (acute, chronic, genotoxicity, carcinogenicity, reproductive/developmental tox, neurotox, immunotox, etc.).
-   Establish dose-response relationships.
-   Determine No-Observed-Adverse-Effect-Level (NOAEL) and Lowest-Observed-Adverse-Effect-Level (LOAEL).
-   Assess risk to human health or environment.
-   Support regulatory decisions.

**Types of Toxicity Tests:**

1.  **Acute Toxicity Testing:**
    *   **Purpose:** Effects shortly after single/short-term exposure (immediate hazard).
    *   **Methods:** LD50/LC50 (*in vivo*, rodents/aquatic organisms - historical, often replaced), dose range finding, clinical observations, pathology.
    *   **Endpoints:** Mortality, clinical signs, gross pathology.
    *   **Duration:** Short-term (24h - 14 days).

2.  **Subchronic Toxicity Testing:**
    *   **Purpose:** Effects from repeated/continuous exposure over weeks/months (target organ toxicity).
    *   **Methods:** Repeated dose *in vivo* studies (rodents, non-rodents - dogs). Multiple doses, control group. Relevant exposure routes.
    *   **Endpoints:** Clinical signs, body weight, food consumption, hematology, clinical chemistry, organ weights, gross pathology, histopathology. NOAEL, LOAEL.
    *   **Duration:** Weeks to months (e.g., 28 days, 90 days).

3.  **Chronic Toxicity Testing:**
    *   **Purpose:** Effects from long-term exposure over significant lifespan portion (cumulative toxicity, carcinogenicity).
    *   **Methods:** Long-term *in vivo* studies (rodent carcinogenicity bioassays - 2 years; chronic toxicity studies - 1 year). Multiple doses, control group.
    *   **Endpoints:** Similar to subchronic, plus carcinogenicity endpoints (tumor data). NOAEL, LOAEL.
    *   **Duration:** Months to years (e.g., 1 year, 2 years).

4.  **Genotoxicity Testing:**
    *   **Purpose:** Potential to damage DNA and cause mutations (relevant to carcinogenicity, heritable damage).
    *   **Methods:** Battery of *in vitro* and *in vivo* assays:
        -   ***In Vitro:*** Ames Test, Chromosome Aberration Assay, Micronucleus Assay, Mouse Lymphoma Assay (MLA)/HPRT Assay.
        -   ***In Vivo:*** Micronucleus Assay (rodent bone marrow/blood), Chromosome Aberration Assay (rodent bone marrow), Comet Assay (DNA strand breaks).
    *   **Endpoints:** Mutagenicity, clastogenicity, aneugenicity, DNA damage.
    *   **Duration:** Short-term exposure.

5.  **Reproductive and Developmental Toxicity Testing (DART):**
    *   **Purpose:** Effects on reproduction (fertility) and development (embryotoxicity, teratogenicity, postnatal effects).
    *   **Methods:** *In vivo* studies (rodents, rabbits):
        -   Fertility & Early Embryonic Development (Segment I).
        -   Embryo-Fetal Development (Teratogenicity - Segment II).
        -   Pre- & Postnatal Development (Segment III).
    *   **Endpoints:** Fertility effects, embryo/fetal toxicity (lethality, malformations, growth retardation), developmental delays, postnatal effects, maternal toxicity. NOAEL, LOAEL.
    *   **Duration:** Exposure during critical periods (mating, pregnancy, lactation).

6.  **Neurotoxicity Testing:**
    *   **Purpose:** Effects on the nervous system (CNS, PNS).
    *   **Methods:** *In vivo* studies (rodents): Behavioral tests (FOB, motor activity, learning/memory), Neuropathology, Electrophysiology.
    *   **Endpoints:** Behavioral changes, neurological deficits, neuropathology, electrophysiological alterations. NOAEL, LOAEL.
    *   **Duration:** Acute, subchronic, or chronic.

7.  **Immunotoxicity Testing:**
    *   **Purpose:** Effects on the immune system (immunosuppression, immunostimulation, hypersensitivity, autoimmunity).
    *   **Methods:** *In vivo* and *in vitro* assays:
        -   ***In Vivo:*** Lymphoid organ histopathology, humoral/cell-mediated immunity assays (antibody response, DTH, T-cell proliferation, CTL), NK cell activity, host resistance assays.
        -   ***In Vitro:*** Lymphocyte proliferation, cytokine production, flow cytometry.
    *   **Endpoints:** Immunosuppression/stimulation, hypersensitivity, autoimmunity, altered immune cell populations/function. NOAEL, LOAEL.
    *   **Duration:** Acute, subchronic, or chronic.

8.  **Other Specialized Tests:** Dermal/Ocular toxicity, Respiratory/Inhalation toxicity, Cardiotoxicity, Hepatotoxicity, Nephrotoxicity, Endocrine Disruption assays, etc.

**Regulatory Requirements:**
Fundamental requirement for pharmaceuticals, pesticides, chemicals (FDA, EPA, EMA). Specific tests depend on product type/exposure. Tiered approach often used (*in vitro* screen -> *in vivo*). Data used for risk assessment, safe exposure levels, labeling, regulation. Must follow Good Laboratory Practices (GLP).
