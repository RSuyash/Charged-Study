# High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC)

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) is a high-resolution form of [[2.3.2 Chromatography|liquid chromatography]]. It utilizes specialized columns packed with small particle size stationary phases and high-pressure pumps to achieve efficient and rapid separations.

![Fig. High Performance Liquid Chromatography](<../../../V2/Industrial Biotechnology/IB-Unit-II/image 25.png>)
*Fig. High Performance Liquid Chromatography*

## Principle

Separation is based on differential migration of sample components through a column due to their different affinities for the stationary and mobile phases.

## Equipment

-   **Pump:** High-pressure pump for controlled mobile phase delivery.
-   **Injector:** Sample injector.
-   **Column:** Packed with stationary phase, designed for high pressure.
-   **Detector:** Detects/quantifies components (UV-Vis, fluorescence, refractive index, mass spectrometer - LC-MS).
-   **Data System:** Controls HPLC, acquires data, analyzes chromatograms.

## HPLC Modes Used for Purification

-   **Reversed-Phase HPLC (RP-HPLC):** Most common for bioproduct purification.
    -   *Stationary Phase:* Non-polar (C18, C8, C4 bonded silica).
    -   *Mobile Phase:* Polar (water-acetonitrile/methanol mixtures with buffers).
    -   *Separation:* Based on hydrophobicity. More hydrophobic molecules retained longer, eluting with increasing organic solvent.
    *   *Applications:* Peptide/protein purification, separation of hydrophobic compounds, analysis of drugs/pharmaceuticals.
-   **Ion-Exchange HPLC (IEX-HPLC):** High-resolution IEX using HPLC columns.
    -   *Stationary Phase:* Strong/weak cation/anion exchange resins.
    -   *Mobile Phase:* Buffer with pH and salt gradients.
    *   *Applications:* High-resolution protein/antibody/nucleic acid purification.
-   **Size-Exclusion HPLC (SE-HPLC) / Gel Filtration HPLC:** High-resolution size-exclusion using HPLC columns.
    *   *Stationary Phase:* Porous gel matrix with controlled pore sizes.
    *   *Mobile Phase:* Isocratic buffer.
    *   *Applications:* Separation based on size, protein purification (monomers from aggregates), polysaccharide purification.
-   **Affinity HPLC:** High-resolution affinity chromatography using HPLC columns.
    *   *Stationary Phase:* Affinity resin with immobilized ligand (antibody, enzyme substrate, metal ion).
    *   *Mobile Phase:* Buffer with elution conditions to disrupt affinity binding.
    *   *Applications:* High-selectivity purification of proteins, enzymes, antibodies.

## Applications

-   High-resolution purification of bioproducts (proteins, peptides, oligonucleotides, drugs, natural products).
-   Analytical HPLC for quality control, purity analysis, quantification.

## Advantages

-   High resolution separation.
-   High efficiency.
-   Rapid separations.
-   Quantitative analysis.
-   Versatile for different separation modes.
-   Automated operation.

## Limitations

-   Higher cost (equipment, columns) compared to conventional chromatography.
-   Lower sample loading capacity.
-   Mobile phase solvents can be expensive.
-   Requires skilled operators.
-   Preparative HPLC (large-scale) can be expensive.

HPLC is a powerful tool for bioproduct purification and analysis, offering high resolution and automation.
