# 2.2 Cell Disruption

Cell disruption (cell lysis or breakage) is a crucial step in [[Downstream Processing]] when the target product is **intracellular**. It releases the product (enzymes, proteins, nucleic acids, inclusion bodies) from the cell matrix into a soluble form for subsequent purification.

The choice of method depends on cell type, cell wall strength, scale, and product sensitivity.

Key methods include:

-   [[2.2.1 Physical Methods of Cell Disruption]]
-   [[2.2.2 Chemical Methods of Cell Disruption]]
-   [[2.2.3 Enzymatic Methods of Cell Disruption]]

Effective cell disruption maximizes product release while minimizing degradation and release of unwanted cellular components.
