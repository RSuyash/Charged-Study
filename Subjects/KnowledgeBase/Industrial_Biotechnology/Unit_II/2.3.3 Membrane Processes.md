# 2.3.3 Membrane Processes

Membrane processes are pressure-driven separation techniques using semi-permeable membranes to separate molecules based on size, shape, or charge. They are widely used in [[Downstream Processing]] for concentration, purification, and separation of bioproducts.

Key membrane processes include:

-   [[Ultrafiltration (UF)]]
-   [[Reverse Osmosis (RO)]]

These processes are versatile and can be applied at various stages, including cell mass removal, clarification, concentration, desalting, and buffer exchange.
