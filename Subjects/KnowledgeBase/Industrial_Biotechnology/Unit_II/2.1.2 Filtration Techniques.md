# 2.1.2 Filtration Techniques

[[Filtration]] is a widely used technique for separating cell mass from fermentation broth based on particle size differences. It involves passing the broth through a porous medium (filter) that retains solid particles (cells, debris) while allowing the liquid (filtrate) containing the product to pass through.

Key types of filtration used for cell mass removal include:

-   [[Depth Filtration]]
-   [[Surface Filtration]] (Membrane Filtration - Microfiltration and Ultrafiltration)

Filtration is a versatile method applicable to various cell types and broth characteristics and can be used for both primary cell mass removal and clarification in [[Downstream Processing]].
