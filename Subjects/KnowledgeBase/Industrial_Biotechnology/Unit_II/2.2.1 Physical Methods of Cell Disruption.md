# 2.2.1 Physical Methods of Cell Disruption

[[2.2 Cell Disruption|Physical methods]] utilize mechanical forces to break open cells for releasing intracellular products. They are widely used due to their broad applicability and scalability.

Key physical methods include:

-   [[Sonication (Ultrasonication)]]
-   [[Bead Milling (Mechanical Cell Disruption using Beads)]]
-   [[High-Pressure Homogenization]]
-   French Press (High-Pressure Extrusion) *(Note: Not explicitly linked in source, but a related method)*
-   Liquid Homogenization (High-Shear Mixing) *(Note: Not explicitly linked in source, but a related method)*

These methods offer alternatives for cell lysis, each with different levels of energy input and suitability for various cell types and scales.
