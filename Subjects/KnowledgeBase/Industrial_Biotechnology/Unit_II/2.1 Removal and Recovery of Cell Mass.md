# 2.1 Removal and Recovery of Cell Mass

Removal and recovery of cell mass is often the first crucial step in [[Downstream Processing]] (DSP) for bioproducts. It separates solid biomass (cells, debris) from the liquid broth containing the desired product. The choice of technique depends on product location (intracellular vs. extracellular), broth characteristics, and scale.

Key methods include:

-   [[2.1.1 Precipitation Methods]]
-   [[2.1.2 Filtration Techniques]]
-   [[2.1.3 Centrifugation Methods]]

This step is essential for concentrating the product and preparing it for subsequent purification steps.
