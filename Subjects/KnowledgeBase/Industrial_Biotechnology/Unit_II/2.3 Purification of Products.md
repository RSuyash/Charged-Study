# 2.3 Purification of Products

After [[2.1 Removal and Recovery of Cell Mass|cell mass removal]] and [[2.2 Cell Disruption|cell disruption]] (if needed), the next major stage in [[Downstream Processing]] is product purification. The goal is to isolate and concentrate the desired product from the complex mixture of cellular components and fermentation broth, achieving the required purity level.

Purification typically involves a series of separation and fractionation steps that exploit differences in physicochemical properties between the target product and impurities.

Key purification techniques include:

-   [[2.3.1 Liquid-Liquid Extraction (Solvent Extraction)]]
-   [[2.3.2 Chromatography]]
-   [[2.3.3 Membrane Processes]]

The specific purification strategy depends on the nature of the product, the impurities present, the desired purity level, and economic considerations.
