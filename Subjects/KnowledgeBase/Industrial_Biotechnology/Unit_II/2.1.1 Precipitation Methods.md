# 2.1.1 Precipitation Methods

Precipitation is a technique used in [[2.1 Removal and Recovery of Cell Mass|cell mass removal]] by altering the solubility of cellular components, causing them to aggregate and settle out of solution. It is a relatively simple and cost-effective method often used as a preliminary step.

Key precipitation methods include:

-   [[Isoelectric Precipitation]]
-   [[Salt-Induced Precipitation (Salting Out)]]
-   [[Solvent Precipitation]]
-   [[Polyelectrolyte Precipitation (Flocculation)]]

Precipitation is generally non-selective, precipitating a mixture of cellular components. Further purification steps are typically required.
