# 1.3 Mutants Insensitive to Inhibitors or Repressors

This section discusses mutants that exhibit insensitivity to inhibitors (molecules decreasing enzyme activity) or repressors (proteins decreasing gene expression). Understanding the mechanisms of action and developing methods to isolate such mutants is important for strain improvement, particularly for overcoming limitations imposed by inhibitory compounds or regulatory circuits.
