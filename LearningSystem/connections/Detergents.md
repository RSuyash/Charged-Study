# Detergents

Detergents are cleaning products composed of surfactants that lower the surface tension of water, allowing it to spread more easily and remove dirt and grease.

## Proteases in Detergents

Alkaline proteases, particularly subtilisins, are key components of laundry detergents and dishwashing detergents. They aid in removing protein-based stains (blood, grass, food) from fabrics and dishes.
