# Protease Connections and Networks in Industrial Biotechnology

## Microorganisms Involved in Protease Production

*   **Bacteria:**
    *   *Bacillus subtilis* and other *Bacillus* species (alkaline proteases, neutral proteases, subtilisins)
    *   *Pseudomonas* species
*   **Fungi:**
    *   *Aspergillus niger* and other *Aspergillus* species (acid proteases, metalloproteases)
    *   *Trichoderma reesei*
*   **Actinomycetes:**
    *   *Streptomyces griseus* and other *Streptomyces* species

## Fermentation Process for Protease Production

*   **Types:**
    *   Submerged Fermentation (SmF)
    *   Solid-State Fermentation (SSF)
*   **Submerged Fermentation (SmF):**
    *   **Raw Materials/Substrates:**
        *   **Carbon Source:** Carbohydrates (glucose, starch, molasses), oils (vegetable oils, animal fats), glycerol
        *   **Nitrogen Source:**
            *   Organic: Soybean meal, casein, corn steep liquor, yeast extract, peptone, meat extract, gelatin
            *   Inorganic: Ammonium salts (ammonium sulfate, ammonium nitrate)
        *   **Minerals:** Phosphorus, magnesium, calcium, iron, zinc, manganese
        *   **Growth Factors:** Vitamins, amino acids
    *   **Fermentation Conditions:**
        *   **Type:** Batch, Fed-Batch
        *   **Aeration:** Aerobic
        *   **Temperature:** 25-37°C (*Bacillus*, *Pseudomonas*), 25-30°C (*Aspergillus*, *Trichoderma*)
        *   **pH:** 7.0-8.5 (bacterial alkaline proteases), 5.0-7.0 (fungal acid proteases)
        *   **Agitation:** Mixing, oxygen distribution, temperature uniformity
    *   **Process Parameters and Control:**
        *   Dissolved Oxygen (DO)
        *   Temperature Control
        *   pH Control
        *   Nutrient Feeding Rate (Fed-Batch)
        *   Foam Control
        *   Monitoring and Control Systems (SCADA)
*   **Solid-State Fermentation (SSF):**
    *   **Substrates:**
        *   Agricultural Residues: Wheat bran, rice bran, corn bran, sugarcane bagasse, soybean meal, oil cakes, straws
        *   Moistening Agents: Water, mineral salt solutions, nutrient solutions
    *   **Fermentation Conditions:**
        *   Aerobic
        *   Temperature Control
        *   Moisture Control
        *   pH Control (Less Common)
    *   **Process Parameters and Control:**
        *   Moisture Content
        *   Aeration
        *   Temperature
        *   Substrate Particle Size and Bed Porosity
        *   Incubation Time

## Product Recovery and Applications of Protease

*   **Product Recovery (Downstream Processing):**
    1.  Cell Mass Removal (Centrifugation, Filtration)
    2.  Product Concentration (Ultrafiltration, Precipitation)
    3.  Purification (Affinity Chromatography, Ion-Exchange Chromatography, Size-Exclusion Chromatography)
    4.  Formulation and Stabilization (Drying, Formulation with stabilizers)
*   **Applications:**
    *   **Detergents:** Alkaline proteases (subtilisins) for stain removal
    *   **Food Industry:**
        *   Baking (dough improvers)
        *   Meat Tenderization (papain, bromelain, ficin)
        *   Cheese Making (rennin/chymosin)
        *   Brewing (haze prevention)
    *   **Leather Processing:** Dehairing, bating
    *   **Textile Industry:** Biopolishing, desizing, bio-scouring
    *   **Pharmaceutical Industry:** Therapeutic enzymes, enzyme-based pharmaceuticals
    *   **Bioremediation:** Degrading proteinaceous waste
    *   **Research and Diagnostics:** Protein analysis, peptide synthesis, cell lysis
