# Glycosides

## Overview

Glycosides are a ubiquitous class of secondary metabolites found throughout the plant kingdom. They are defined by the presence of a **glycosidic bond**, which links a sugar moiety (**glycone**) to a non-sugar component (**aglycone**). This glycosylation often modifies the physicochemical properties (like solubility) and biological activity of the aglycone.

## Structure and Classification

-   **Glycone (Sugar Moiety):** Typically a monosaccharide (e.g., D-glucose, D-galactose, L-rhamnose) or an oligosaccharide. Glucose is common (glucosides).
-   **Aglycone (Genin or Non-Sugar Moiety):** The non-sugar component, which dictates the type and activity. Can be terpenoids, steroids, flavonoids, alkaloids, etc.
-   **Glycosidic Bond:** Links glycone and aglycone (O-, N-, S-, or C-glycosidic bond). O-glycosidic bonds are most common.

## Types and Prominent Examples

-   **Cardiac Glycosides:** Steroidal ([[Terpenoids|terpenoid]]) aglycone, affect the heart (e.g., Digoxin from *Digitalis purpurea*). Inhibit Na+/K+ ATPase pump.
-   **Flavonoid Glycosides:** Flavonoid ([[Phenolic Compounds|phenolic]]) aglycone, often enhance solubility/stability (e.g., Rutin, Quercetin glycosides, Anthocyanins). Antioxidant properties.
-   **Anthraquinone Glycosides:** Anthraquinone ([[Polyketides|polyketide-derived]]) aglycone, laxative properties (e.g., Senna glycosides from *Senna alexandrina*, Aloe-emodin glycosides from *Aloe vera*).
-   **Cyanogenic Glycosides:** Aglycone derived from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Amino Acid Derived Pathways|amino acids]], release hydrogen cyanide (HCN) upon hydrolysis, defense mechanism (e.g., Amygdalin from bitter almonds, Linamarin from flax).
-   **Glucosinolates:** Sulfur-containing S-glycosides derived from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Amino Acid Derived Pathways|amino acids]], found in *Brassicaceae* (e.g., Sulforaphane from broccoli). Produce pungent compounds upon hydrolysis.
-   **Saponins:** (Often classified separately) Glycosides with triterpenoid or steroid ([[Terpenoids|terpenoid]]) aglycones, exhibit surfactant properties.
