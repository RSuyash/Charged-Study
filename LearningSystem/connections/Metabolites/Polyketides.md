# Polyketides

## Overview

Polyketides represent an extraordinarily diverse class of secondary metabolites, characterized by their biosynthesis via **polyketide synthases (PKSs)**, which utilize the [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Malonyl-CoA Pathway (Polyketide Synthesis)|Malonyl-CoA Pathway]]. PKSs assemble polyketide chains from simple acyl-CoA building blocks through decarboxylative condensation reactions, similar to fatty acid synthesis but with greater structural versatility. They are primarily produced by fungi and bacteria.

## Biosynthesis via Polyketide Synthases (PKSs)

-   **PKS Types:**
    -   **Type I PKSs:** Large, modular enzymes (fungi, bacteria) producing complex macrocycles (e.g., erythromycin, amphotericin B).
    -   **Type II PKSs:** Dissociated enzyme systems (bacteria) producing aromatic polyketides (e.g., tetracyclines, anthracyclines).
    -   **Type III PKSs:** Simpler, single-domain enzymes (plants, some microbes) often involved in flavonoid biosynthesis.
-   **Building Blocks:** Use "starter" (e.g., acetyl-CoA) and "extender" (e.g., malonyl-CoA) units.
-   **Structural Diversity:** Linear, branched, aromatic, macrocyclic, polycyclic structures result from variations in building blocks and enzymatic modifications.

## Pharmacologically Important Polyketides

-   **Antibiotics:**
    -   *Macrolides*: Erythromycin, Azithromycin (inhibit 50S ribosome).
    -   *Tetracyclines*: Tetracycline, Doxycycline (inhibit 30S ribosome).
    -   *Anthracyclines*: Doxorubicin, Daunorubicin (anticancer antibiotics, intercalate DNA).
-   **Antifungals:**
    -   *Polyenes*: Amphotericin B, Nystatin (bind ergosterol in fungal membranes).
-   **Immunosuppressants:**
    -   *Rapamycin (Sirolimus)*: Inhibits mTOR.
    -   *Cyclosporine* (biosynthesis involves PKS-NRPS hybrid systems): Inhibits calcineurin.
-   **Cholesterol-Lowering Agents:**
    -   *Statins*: Lovastatin, Simvastatin (inhibit HMG-CoA reductase).
-   **Natural Pigments:**
    -   *Anthraquinones*: Pigments, laxatives, dyes.
