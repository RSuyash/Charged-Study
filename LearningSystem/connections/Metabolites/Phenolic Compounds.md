# Phenolic Compounds

## Overview

Phenolic compounds are a diverse group of secondary metabolites characterized by the presence of one or more hydroxyl (-OH) groups attached to an aromatic hydrocarbon ring. These compounds are widely distributed in the plant kingdom and play significant roles in plant physiology, ecology, and human health.

## Structure

Phenolic compounds can be classified based on their structure into several categories:

1.  **Simple Phenols**: Single aromatic ring with hydroxyl groups (e.g., phenol, catechol).
2.  **Phenolic Acids**: Aromatic acids with a phenolic ring and carboxyl group.
    -   *Hydroxybenzoic Acids*: e.g., salicylic acid, gallic acid.
    -   *Hydroxycinnamic Acids*: e.g., caffeic acid, ferulic acid.
3.  **Flavonoids**: Large group with C6-C3-C6 structure (e.g., flavonols like quercetin, flavanones, anthocyanins).
4.  **Tannins**: High molecular weight compounds that precipitate proteins (hydrolyzable and condensed tannins).
5.  **Lignins**: Complex polymers providing structural support in plant cell walls.

## Biosynthesis

Synthesized through several pathways:

-   **[[Biosynthetic Pathways/Shikimic Acid Pathway|Shikimic Acid Pathway]]**: Produces aromatic amino acids (e.g., phenylalanine), precursors for many phenolics.
-   **Phenylpropanoid Pathway**: Derives from the [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Shikimic Acid Pathway|Shikimic Acid Pathway]], leads to flavonoids, lignins, etc.
-   **Acetate-Malonate Pathway** (related to [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Malonyl-CoA Pathway (Polyketide Synthesis)|Malonyl-CoA Pathway]] via Type III PKS): Contributes to some flavonoids and complex phenolics.

## Sources

Abundant in various plant sources:

-   Fruits (e.g., apples, berries) → Flavonoids, phenolic acids.
-   Vegetables (e.g., onions, spinach) → Phenolic compounds.
-   Beverages (e.g., tea, coffee) → Polyphenols.

## Examples

1.  **Flavonoids**:
    -   *Quercetin*: Antioxidant (onions, apples).
    -   *Anthocyanins*: Pigments, antioxidant (berries).
2.  **Phenolic Acids**:
    -   *Caffeic Acid*: Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory (coffee).
    -   *Ferulic Acid*: Antioxidant (whole grains).
3.  **Tannins**:
    -   *Grape Tannins*: Astringency, potential health benefits (wine).

## Functions in Plants

-   **Defense Mechanisms**: Phytoalexins against pathogens and herbivores.
-   **UV Protection**: Flavonoids absorb UV radiation.
-   **Attractants for Pollinators**: Contribute to flower color and scent.

## Medicinal Applications

-   **Antioxidant Activity**: Neutralize free radicals, potentially reducing chronic disease risk.
-   **Anti-inflammatory Effects**: e.g., curcumin (turmeric).
-   **Antimicrobial Properties**: Against bacteria and fungi.
-   **Cardiovascular Health**: Polyphenols associated with improved cardiovascular health.
