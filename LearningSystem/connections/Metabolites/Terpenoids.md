# Terpenoids

## Overview

Terpenoids, also known as isoprenoids, are a large and diverse class of naturally occurring organic compounds derived from **five-carbon isoprene units**. They are characterized by their diverse structures and biological functions, playing critical roles in plant defense mechanisms, attraction of pollinators, and ecological interactions.

## Structure

Terpenoids can be classified based on the number of isoprene units they contain:

- **Monoterpenes (C10)**: Composed of two isoprene units (e.g., limonene, menthol).
- **Sesquiterpenes (C15)**: Composed of three isoprene units (e.g., farnesene, artemisinin).
- **Diterpenes (C20)**: Composed of four isoprene units (e.g., taxol).
- **Triterpenes (C30)**: Composed of six isoprene units (e.g., squalene).
- **Tetraterpenes (C40)**: Composed of eight isoprene units (e.g., carotenoids).

## Sources

Terpenoids are widely distributed across various plant families, particularly those producing essential oils. Notable sources include:

- *Mentha piperita* (peppermint) → menthol
- *Citrus limon* (lemon) → limonene
- *Artemisia annua* (sweet wormwood) → artemisinin
- *Taxus brevifolia* (Pacific yew) → paclitaxel (Taxol)

## Examples and Pharmacological Activities

1.  **Artemisinin**: [[Biological Activity/Antimalarial Activity|Antimalarial agent]].
2.  **Taxol (Paclitaxel)**: [[Biological Activity/Anticancer Activity|Anticancer drug]].
3.  **Limonene**: [[Biological Activity/Anti-inflammatory Activity|Anti-inflammatory]], [[Biological Activity/Antioxidant Activity|antioxidant]], potential [[Biological Activity/Anticancer Activity|anticancer]].
4.  **Squalene**: Emollient, immunomodulatory.
5.  **Geraniol**: [[Biological Activity/Antimicrobial Activity|Antimicrobial]], fragrance.

## Biosynthesis

Occurs through two primary pathways:

1.  **[[Biosynthetic Pathways/Mevalonate Pathway (MVA)|Mevalonate pathway]]**: Produces sterols and triterpenes (cytoplasmic).
2.  **[[Biosynthetic Pathways/Methylerythritol Phosphate Pathway (MEP)|Methylerythritol phosphate (MEP) pathway]]**: Produces monoterpenes, sesquiterpenes, etc. (plastidial/bacterial).

## Medicinal Applications

- **Anticancer Agents**: e.g., paclitaxel.
- **Antimicrobial Properties**: Many terpenoids exhibit activity against bacteria, fungi, and viruses.
- **Anti-inflammatory Effects**: e.g., limonene.
- **Flavoring Agents and Aromatherapy**: Contribute to flavors and scents.
