# Coumarins

## Overview

Coumarins are a large family of aromatic plant secondary metabolites, derived via the Phenylpropanoid pathway originating from the [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Shikimic Acid Pathway|Shikimic Acid Pathway]], defined by the **benzo-α-pyrone** (or benzopyran-2-one) ring system. These compounds are widespread in the plant kingdom and are known for their diverse biological activities, including anticoagulant, photosensitizing, antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory effects.

## Structure and Classification

-   **Benzo-α-Pyrone Ring System:** Basic structure is a benzene ring fused to an α-pyrone ring.
-   **Substituent Diversity:** Variations in substituents (hydroxyl, methoxy, isoprenyl, glycosidic groups) lead to diversity.
-   **Classification:**
    -   *Simple Coumarins*: Basic structure with simple substituents (e.g., umbelliferone).
    -   *Furanocoumarins*: Fused furan ring, often photosensitizing (e.g., psoralen).
    -   *Pyranocoumarins*: Fused pyran ring (e.g., xanthyletin).
    -   *Isocoumarins*: Isomers with different ring fusion.
    -   *Dimeric Coumarins*: Formed from two coumarin units.

## Biological Activities and Applications

-   **Anticoagulant Activity:** Some coumarins (e.g., Dicoumarol, Warfarin) act as vitamin K antagonists, inhibiting clotting factor synthesis. Used therapeutically (Warfarin).
-   **Photosensitization (Furanocoumarins):** Activated by UVA light, form DNA adducts. Used in PUVA therapy (Psoralens) for skin conditions. Can cause phytophotodermatitis.
-   **Antimicrobial and Antifungal Activities:** Active against various bacteria and fungi.
-   **Antioxidant and Anti-inflammatory Properties:** Scavenge free radicals, inhibit pro-inflammatory enzymes.
-   **Other Activities:** Anticancer, antiviral, neuroprotective effects under investigation.

## Plant Sources

Widely distributed in plants, including families like *Fabaceae* (legumes), *Apiaceae* (carrots), *Rutaceae* (citrus), *Asteraceae* (sunflowers). Sweet clover (*Melilotus officinalis*) is a notable source.
