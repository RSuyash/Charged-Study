# Research and Diagnostics

Research and diagnostics encompass a wide range of activities aimed at understanding biological processes and identifying diseases or conditions.

## Proteases in Research and Diagnostics

Proteases are widely used in research laboratories and diagnostic kits for protein analysis, peptide synthesis, cell lysis, and various biochemical assays.
