# Textile Industry

The textile industry is concerned with the design, production, and distribution of yarn, cloth, and clothing.

## Proteases in Textile Industry

Proteases are used in textile processing for biopolishing (removing fuzz and pills from fabrics), desizing (removing starch coatings), and bio-scouring (removing impurities from cotton).
