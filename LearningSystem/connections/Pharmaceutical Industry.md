# Pharmaceutical Industry

The pharmaceutical industry discovers, develops, produces, and markets drugs or pharmaceutical drugs for use as medications to be administered (or self-administered) to patients, with the aim to cure them, vaccinate them, or alleviate the symptoms.

## Proteases in the Pharmaceutical Industry

Proteases have various applications in the pharmaceutical industry, including:

*   Therapeutic Enzymes: Some proteases (e.g., trypsin, chymotrypsin, bromelain, serratiopeptidase) are used as therapeutic enzymes for wound debridement, anti-inflammatory agents, and digestive aids.
*   Enzyme-Based Pharmaceuticals: Proteases are used in the production of certain peptide and protein drugs.
