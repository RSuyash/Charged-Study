# Meroterpenoids and Prenylation

This note describes secondary metabolites with mixed biosynthetic origins, specifically those combining **isoprenoid (terpenoid)** units with structures derived from other pathways (e.g., polyketide, shikimate, amino acid).

## Definitions

*   **Prenylation:** The enzymatic attachment of hydrophobic isoprenoid groups (like farnesyl or geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate, derived from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Mevalonate Pathway (MVA)]] or [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Methylerythritol Phosphate Pathway (MEP)]]) to acceptor molecules, often proteins or other secondary metabolites.
*   **Meroterpenoids:** A class of natural products having a partial terpenoid structure. They are hybrid molecules derived from both terpenoid precursors and precursors from other pathways like [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Malonyl-CoA Pathway (Polyketide Synthesis)|polyketide]], [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Shikimic Acid Pathway|shikimate]], or [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Amino Acid Derived Pathways|amino acid]] routes.

## Significance

*   **Structural Diversity:** Prenylation dramatically increases the structural diversity and complexity of natural products.
*   **Modified Properties:** The attached isoprenoid chain often modifies the lipophilicity, membrane association, and biological activity of the parent molecule.
*   **Bioactivity:** Many meroterpenoids exhibit significant biological activities, including antimicrobial, anticancer, and anti-inflammatory effects.

## Examples

*   **Prenylated Flavonoids:** [[Phenolic Compounds|Flavonoids]] with attached isoprenoid chains (e.g., Icaritin).
*   **Ergot Alkaloids:** Complex [[Alkaloids]] derived from tryptophan, dimethylallyl pyrophosphate (DMAPP - a terpenoid precursor), and methionine.
*   **Some Polyketide Antibiotics:** Certain [[Polyketides]] undergo prenylation.
*   **Vitamin E (Tocopherols):** Chromanol ring (phenolic-like, from shikimate pathway) with a phytyl (terpenoid) side chain.
*   **Cannabinoids (e.g., THC, CBD):** Often considered meroterpenoids, combining olivetolic acid (polyketide-derived) with geranyl pyrophosphate (terpenoid).

## Connections

*   Structurally links [[Terpenoids]] with [[Phenolic Compounds]], [[Alkaloids]], and [[Polyketides]].
*   Represents a key mechanism for generating chemical diversity in secondary metabolism.
