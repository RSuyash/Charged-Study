# Anticancer Activity

This note describes the biological activity of compounds used in the treatment of cancer (antineoplastic agents). These compounds interfere with the growth, proliferation, or survival of cancer cells through various mechanisms. Many important chemotherapy drugs are derived from or inspired by [[Secondary Metabolites]].

## Mechanisms of Action

Anticancer agents target various aspects of cancer cell biology:
*   **DNA Damage/Interference:** Intercalating into DNA, causing strand breaks, or inhibiting DNA replication/repair enzymes (e.g., Topoisomerase inhibitors).
*   **Microtubule Disruption:** Interfering with the formation or breakdown of microtubules, essential for cell division (mitosis).
*   **Inhibition of Signaling Pathways:** Blocking pathways crucial for cancer cell growth and survival.
*   **Induction of Apoptosis:** Triggering programmed cell death.
*   **Anti-angiogenesis:** Inhibiting the formation of new blood vessels that supply tumors.

## Secondary Metabolite Classes with Anticancer Activity

*   **[[Alkaloids]]:** (from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Amino Acid Derived Pathways|Amino Acid Derived Pathways]]) A significant source of anticancer drugs.
    *   *Vinca Alkaloids* (e.g., Vincristine, Vinblastine): Inhibit microtubule polymerization. Derived from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Amino Acid Derived Pathways|amino acids]].
    *   *Taxanes* (e.g., Paclitaxel/Taxol - technically a diterpenoid, but often discussed alongside alkaloids in this context): Stabilize microtubules, preventing breakdown. Derived from [[Terpenoids]].
    *   *Camptothecins* (e.g., Topotecan, Irinotecan - semi-synthetic derivatives): Inhibit Topoisomerase I. Derived from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Amino Acid Derived Pathways|amino acids]].
*   **[[Terpenoids]]:** (from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Mevalonate Pathway (MVA)|MVA]] / [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Methylerythritol Phosphate Pathway (MEP)|MEP]] Pathways)
    *   *Taxanes* (Paclitaxel/Taxol): See above.
    *   Other terpenoids show potential or are under investigation.
*   **[[Polyketides]]:** (from [[Biosynthetic Pathways/Malonyl-CoA Pathway (Polyketide Synthesis)|Malonyl-CoA Pathway]])
    *   *Anthracyclines* (e.g., Doxorubicin, Daunorubicin): Intercalate DNA and inhibit Topoisomerase II.
    *   *Epothilones*: Microtubule stabilizers (similar mechanism to Taxol).
*   **[[Phenolic Compounds]]:** Some flavonoids and other phenolics show anticancer potential in preclinical studies through antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, or pathway inhibition mechanisms.
*   **[[Glycosides]]:** Activity depends on the aglycone; some anticancer agents are glycosides (e.g., Doxorubicin is an anthracycline glycoside).

## Significance

*   **Medicine:** Chemotherapy remains a cornerstone of cancer treatment.
*   **Drug Discovery:** Natural products continue to be a vital source of new anticancer leads.
