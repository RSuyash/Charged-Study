# Plant Sources of Secondary Metabolites

This note details the various plant species that produce significant secondary metabolites, highlighting their importance in medicine, ecology, and industry.

## [[Alkaloids]]

- *Papaver somniferum* (Opium Poppy)
  - Morphine
  - Codeine

- *Cinchona officinalis* (Cinchona Bark)
  - Quinine

- *Erythroxylum coca*
  - Cocaine

- *Catharanthus roseus* (Madagascar Periwinkle)
  - Vincristine

- *Galanthus* (Daffodil)
  - Galantamine

## [[Terpenoids]]

- *Mentha piperita* (Peppermint)
  - Menthol

- *Citrus limon* (Lemon)
  - Limonene

- *Artemisia annua* (Sweet Wormwood)
  - Artemisinin

- *Taxus brevifolia* (Pacific Yew)
  - Paclitaxel

## [[Phenolic Compounds]]

- Fruits (apples, berries)
  - Flavonoids

- Vegetables (onions, spinach)
  - Phenolic acids

- Tea and Coffee
  - Polyphenols

## [[Glycosides]]

- *Digitalis purpurea* (Foxglove)
  - Cardiac glycosides

- *Senna alexandrina*
  - Anthraquinone glycosides

- *Aloe vera*
  - Aloe-emodin glycosides

- *Prunus amygdalus* (Bitter almonds)
  - Amygdalin

## [[Essential Oils]]

- *Lavandula* (Lavender)
  - Linalool

- *Eucalyptus*
  - Eucalyptol

- *Mentha* (Peppermint)
  - Menthol

- *Citrus*
  - Limonene
