# Amino Acid Derived Pathways

This represents a broad category of biosynthetic pathways that utilize amino acids as primary precursors for synthesizing a wide range of [[Secondary Metabolites]], most notably the diverse group of [[Alkaloids]]. Unlike the more defined pathways like Shikimic Acid or MVA/MEP, these routes are highly varied depending on the starting amino acid and the target metabolite.

## Key Precursor Amino Acids and Derived Alkaloids

*   **Aromatic Amino Acids (from [[Shikimic Acid Pathway]]):**
    *   **Tryptophan:** Indole alkaloids (e.g., serotonin, psilocybin, vincristine, strychnine, ergot alkaloids - which are also [[Structural Features/Meroterpenoids and Prenylation|meroterpenoids]]).
    *   **Tyrosine:** Isoquinoline alkaloids (e.g., morphine, codeine, papaverine), benzylisoquinoline alkaloids, phenylethylamines (e.g., mescaline, dopamine).
    *   **Phenylalanine:** Ephedrine, pseudoephedrine, contributes to some phenolics.
*   **Aliphatic Amino Acids:**
    *   **Ornithine (derived from Glutamate):** Tropane alkaloids (e.g., cocaine, atropine), pyrrolizidine alkaloids.
    *   **Lysine:** Piperidine alkaloids (e.g., coniine, lobeline), quinolizidine alkaloids (e.g., lupinine).
*   **Other Amino Acids:**
    *   **Histidine:** Imidazole alkaloids (e.g., histamine, pilocarpine).
    *   **Anthranilic Acid (derived from Chorismate via Shikimate Pathway):** Acridine alkaloids, quinoline alkaloids.

## General Reactions Involved

These pathways often involve common enzymatic reactions such as:
*   Decarboxylation
*   Transamination
*   Oxidation/Reduction
*   Methylation (often via S-Adenosyl methionine - SAM)
*   Cyclization
*   [[Glycosides|Glycosylation]]

## Significance

*   **[[Alkaloids]] Production:** Responsible for the synthesis of thousands of structurally diverse alkaloids, many with potent pharmacological activities (medicines, poisons, stimulants).
*   **Ecological Roles:** Alkaloids often serve as defense compounds against herbivores and pathogens.
*   **Other Metabolites:** Amino acids also serve as precursors for glucosinolates, cyanogenic [[Glycosides]], and non-protein amino acids.

## Connections

*   **Upstream:** Linked to primary metabolism (amino acid synthesis). The [[Shikimic Acid Pathway]] is crucial for providing aromatic amino acid precursors.
*   **Downstream:** Leads to the vast array of [[Alkaloids]] and other nitrogen-containing secondary metabolites.
*   **Related Metabolites:** [[Phenolic Compounds]] (some derived from Tyrosine/Phenylalanine), [[Glycosides]].
