# Shikimic Acid Pathway

The Shikimic Acid Pathway is a crucial metabolic route used by bacteria, archaea, fungi, algae, protozoans, and plants for the biosynthesis of folates and aromatic amino acids (phenylalanine, tyrosine, and tryptophan).

## Key Steps & Intermediates

1.  Condensation of **Phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP)** and **Erythrose-4-phosphate (E4P)**.
2.  Formation of **Shikimate**.
3.  Phosphorylation and addition of another PEP molecule to form **Chorismate**.

## Branch Point: Chorismate

Chorismate is a critical branch point intermediate:
*   Leads to **Tryptophan**.
*   Leads to **Phenylalanine** and **Tyrosine** via Prephenate.
*   Serves as a precursor for **Folate** biosynthesis.
*   Precursor for many [[Phenolic Compounds]] like flavonoids, lignans, tannins, and [[Coumarins]] (often via Phenylalanine/Tyrosine).

## Significance

*   **Essential Amino Acids:** Provides aromatic amino acids essential for protein synthesis in organisms possessing the pathway. Animals lack this pathway and must obtain these amino acids from their diet.
*   **[[Secondary Metabolites]] Production:** Generates precursors for a huge diversity of plant [[Phenolic Compounds]] involved in defense, signaling, pigmentation (e.g., anthocyanins), and structural support (lignin).
*   **Herbicide Target:** The enzyme EPSP synthase in this pathway is the target of the widely used herbicide glyphosate.

## Connections

*   **Upstream:** Linked to glycolysis (PEP) and the pentose phosphate pathway (E4P).
*   **Downstream:** Feeds into protein synthesis and the biosynthesis of numerous [[Phenolic Compounds]] and [[Alkaloids]] (some derived from aromatic amino acids).
*   **Related Pathways:** Interconnected with pathways producing [[Coumarins]], Flavonoids, Lignin etc.
