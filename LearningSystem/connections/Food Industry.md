# Food Industry

The food industry is a global collective of diverse businesses that supply most of the food energy consumed by the world's population.

## Proteases in the Food Industry

Proteases are used in various food processing applications, including:

*   Baking (dough improvers)
*   Meat Tenderization (papain, bromelain, ficin)
*   Cheese Making (rennin/chymosin)
*   Brewing (haze prevention)
