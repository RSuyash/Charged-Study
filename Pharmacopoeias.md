# 4.5.1 Introduction to Indian and International Pharmacopoeias

## What is a Pharmacopoeia?

A pharmacopoeia is an officially recognized book that sets forth standards and specifications for drugs and other substances used in medicine. It ensures quality, safety, and efficacy.

## Key Features

*   [[Monographs]]: Detailed descriptions of individual drug substances.
*   [[General Notices]]: Guidance on interpreting pharmacopoeial standards.
*   [[General Chapters]]: Standardized test procedures.

## Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP)

*   The official book of standards for drugs in India.
*   Published by the [[Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission]].

## International Pharmacopoeias

*   [[United States Pharmacopeia (USP)]]
*   [[European Pharmacopoeia (Ph. Eur.)]]
*   [[British Pharmacopoeia (BP)]]
*   [[Japanese Pharmacopoeia (JP)]]
*   [[World Health Organization (WHO) International Pharmacopoeia]]

## Importance

*   Ensuring drug quality and safety.
*   Harmonizing standards for global trade.
*   Providing a legal and regulatory framework for pharmaceuticals.
